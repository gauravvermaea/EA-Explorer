# EA Explorer 5.7.0: Release 5.7, branding, customer journeys and the deck tools

Release 5.7 (built 17 Nov 2026; the owner: "build 5.7"): the views' ArchiMate icons first (a bug), then **logo and
branding**, **customer journey maps**, **a presentation deck with your own AI** through MCP, and the **backup and
restore test on all of it**.

**Before updating:** take a backup (`.\eax backup before-5.7.0`). From 5.6.0 the update runs migrations 0100 to 0103
(branding: two tables; customer journeys: seven tables; the version; fonts for the themes: one table). A **clean rebuild** is needed: the API (it
gains the Pillow library), the screens, the sample data and the backup test change (`.\eax clean-restart`). Then
`.\eax test full`, which ends with the backup and restore test, and the DR drill (`.\dr\eax-drill`).

## Logo and branding (R5.7-B1, D729)

- **System admin › Branding** (Super Admins): upload the organisation's **logo** (shown in the top bar) and a small
  **icon for the browser tab**, and give the **name in the top bar** (EA Explorer then shows small beside it).
- **Sixteen themes ship**: Standard (light, today's look), Dark, High contrast (for accessibility), Corporate (muted),
  and twelve colours: Blue, Red, Green, Teal, Purple, Orange, Rose, Indigo, Gold, Graphite, Navy and Sand. **Choosing a
  theme shows it at once** on your screen so you see how it feels; **Save** makes it permanent.
  Choose the organisation's theme, and switch on whether **each person may pick their own** on My profile › My look.
- **A theme designer**: start from a copy of any theme and change its colours with colour pickers, its fonts (the
  computers' own), text size and corners; every change shows at once, and Save keeps it as a theme of your own.
- **Your own fonts**: upload a WOFF2, WOFF, TrueType or OpenType file under a family name (with its bold and italic
  files if you have them); the theme designer offers it, and every screen loads it from EA Explorer itself.
- **Your own themes as CSS**: download any theme as a CSS file, change its colours, fonts and corners, and add it back
  under a name; replace or remove the ones you added. A theme may hold only looks: anything that loads from elsewhere
  or runs is refused, with the line it is on.
- **Nobody is locked out**: *Standard look* in each person's menu puts the standard look on in that browser, and a
  break-glass session always has it.
- Only EA Explorer's own screens change: the sign-in page, Reports, Documents, draw.io and the files EA Explorer makes
  keep their own look. Branding is in the database, so backups, restores and site copies carry it.
- Sample data: a theme, Meridian teal (sample), added and not chosen.
- Migration 0100 (two tables).

## Customer journey maps (R5.7-J1, D730)

- A project's **Customer journeys** tab, beside the Business model canvas: any number of journeys, each a **grid** of
  stages across and, under each stage, its **steps, touchpoints, pain points, moments that matter and opportunities**;
  **personas** for the whole journey; a cell edited by clicking it.
- **How the person feels**: each step's emotion from **-2 to +2 with faces**, drawn as a **curve** above the steps.
- **Your own kinds of part and fields** (for example *Systems behind it* with an *Owner*), and extra fields on the
  standard kinds.
- **Linked to the models**: a stage or a part links to elements of the project's models (capabilities, value streams,
  business services, applications, data, actors and roles).
- **Versions like the canvas**: current state, target state, working copy, scenario; draft or agreed; **make a copy**
  (the target from today), **compare** two stage by stage (added, changed with the old text and face, removed); copy to
  another project; every change kept.
- **Export** a journey or a comparison as **PDF, PowerPoint or draw.io** (the drawn view for presentations).
- **MCP tools** `customer_journey` and `change_customer_journey`, so your own AI can draft a journey from interviews.
- **Start blank or from the starter template** (six typical stages). Sample data: *Make a motor claim* today and in
  2028 in Protea Claims Transformation 2028, and *Buy pet insurance* from the starter in Fynbos Pet Insurance launch.
- Project backups carry journeys. Migration 0101 (seven tables). Documentation › Customer journeys.

## A presentation deck with your own AI (R5.7-P1, D731)

- Three **MCP tools** so your own AI builds a deck about a project in your own client, in its own look; nothing is
  saved back to EA Explorer:
  - `deck_outline`: a suggested outline from where the project is (the phase now, what is done, what is open);
  - `deck_sources`: everything a deck may draw on (the project, phases, deliverables and their documents, models,
    views, drawn views, decisions, risks, issues, requirements, key questions, sign-offs, stakeholders, canvases and
    journeys), each with its source, so a slide can say where it came from;
  - `view_picture`: a view or drawn view as a **PNG** or an **SVG**, drawn as on the screens with the ArchiMate icons,
    or as its **raw data** to draw your own.
- Only what you may read; a document marked NOT FOR AI USE OR AUTOMATED PROCESSING by its title only.
- The API gains the Pillow library (for the PNG). Documentation › A presentation deck with your own AI.

## The backup and restore test covers 5.7 (R5.7-T1)

- `eax test backup-restore` (and the DR drill, which runs it) now also makes a **customer journey** (from the starter,
  a step with its emotion, a persona, a pain point, a part of a kind added by people with its field, a link to a model
  element, a target-state copy and a comparison), an **uploaded theme** and the test account's **own theme**, and calls
  the **deck tools**. Each is read back, gone after restoring B1 and back exactly after B2; after the restores the deck
  tools read the restored project and the journey still compares with its target.
- The test never changes what everyone sees: the organisation's logo and theme are left alone, and what it made is
  removed at the end.

## Fixed

- **Views show the ArchiMate icons** (R5.7-V1, D728). A view drawn on EA Explorer's screens (a viewpoint's diagram, a
  model's views and an entity's neighbourhood) now draws every object the way draw.io does: the box's ArchiMate
  corners (square, rounded for behaviour and strategy, cut for motivation) and the icon of its type in the top-right
  corner; a grouping is a dashed box. Colours, layout and the layer bands are unchanged, and the picture and PDF
  exports show the icons too. Types with no ArchiMate element stay plain boxes, as in draw.io.
