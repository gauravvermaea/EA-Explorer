Add an attribute to a type

For: Model Admin, Industry model makers

A field on an entity type: text, number, date, list, link.

Why do this

Attributes are the fields of an entity type: owner, cost, lifecycle status. Adding one changes what everyone can record about that type, so it goes through a metamodel draft and review. Choose attributes that answer real questions; each one asks people for effort. See Metamodels: the shape of a model.

A metamodel change ripples: into what people may record, what reports read, what rules check and what integrations send. That is why it goes through drafts and review, and why each version is kept and can be compared.

In TOGAF terms: The content framework and content metamodel: what building blocks and relationships the architecture may hold. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: A type can hold one more piece of information.

Before you start You need the Model Admin role on the model.

Steps

  1. Open the model, Metamodel › Types, and the type.
  2. New attribute: name, kind, required or not, its list of values.
  3. Save.
