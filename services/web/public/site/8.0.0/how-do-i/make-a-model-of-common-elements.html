Make a model of the common elements

For: Model Admin, Practising architects

A new model from what several models have in common.

Why do this

When several models describe overlapping areas (two regions, two acquired companies), the common elements are a candidate standard or shared core. Making a model of them is the first step to a common reference. See Models: what they are and how they relate.

Nothing in EA Explorer is lost: every change is kept, moments can be named, and work can happen in copies that merge back after review. This is what lets many people change one model without fear.

In TOGAF terms: Phase H and the Architecture Repository: change to the baseline is controlled, recorded and reversible. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: What several models share becomes a model of its own.

Steps

  1. Open Architecture Work › Compare models › Common analysis.
  2. Pick the models; What counts: the threshold, types and fields.
  3. Make a model of the common elements: name it; Make it.
