Change content with AI

For: Practising architects

Your AI assistant adds and changes entities and relationships as your uncommitted changes.

On this page
  • Why do this
  • Things to ask
  • What happens

Why do this

Your AI assistant can add and change entities and relationships as you, from what you tell it or from documents you give it. Its changes are your uncommitted changes until reviewed and committed, so nothing it does counts until a person agrees. See AI in EA Explorer.

Using AI in EA Explorer is always your assistant working with your rights: it reads and drafts, you review and commit. Used well, it takes the slow parts of architecture work (searching, cross-checking, first drafts) so you spend your time on judgement.

In TOGAF terms: Outside the standard's method, but in service of it: AI speeds up gathering content (phases B to D), analysis (E) and reporting to stakeholders, with people reviewing every change. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: Your AI assistant drafts changes for you to review and commit.

Before you start Connect your AI assistant. You need the Architect role (or higher) on the model.

Things to ask

  • Read this strategy and create the drivers, goals and outcomes with their links.
  • Load this table of applications into the model.
  • Give every application without an owner the owner of its capability.
  • Make a copy of this model and remove all duplicates as you see fit.

What happens

It shows the changes first and makes them only after your yes. They are your uncommitted changes until committed, like any other; the model is tagged first.
