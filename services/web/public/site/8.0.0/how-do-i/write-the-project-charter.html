Write the project charter

For: Project Admin, Practising architects

Scope, goals, outcomes and the sponsor who approves it.

Why do this

The charter states why the project exists, what it covers, its goals and outcomes, and who sponsors it. It is the foundation the ADM's Architecture Vision builds, and the sponsor approves it. See The ADM: how architecture work is done.

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

In TOGAF terms: Phase A, Architecture Vision: the statement of architecture work the sponsor agrees. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: The project's purpose and scope are agreed with its sponsor.

Before you start You need a role on the project (Architect or higher), unless a step says otherwise.

Steps

  1. Open the project, Charter.
  2. Fill in the scope: breadth, depth, time and what is out of scope.
  3. Add goals, and outcomes with how they are measured.
  4. Pick the sponsor; send it to them for approval.
