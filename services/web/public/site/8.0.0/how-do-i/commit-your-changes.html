Commit your changes

For: Practising architects

Make your uncommitted changes part of the model, for everyone.

On this page
  • Why do this
  • Steps
  • If something goes wrong

Why do this

Changes start as yours alone. Committing them, with a message saying what and why, makes them part of the shared model and part of its history. Review before commit is what lets an organisation trust a model many people change. See Versions, tags and merging.

Content work is the everyday heart of architecture: recording what exists and what should, and how it all connects. EA Explorer checks it against the metamodel as you go and keeps it as your change until it is reviewed.

In TOGAF terms: Phases B, C and D: recording the building blocks and relationships of the baseline and target architectures. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: Your changes become part of the model for everyone.

Before you start You need the Committer role, or a model that lets its Architects commit their own.

Steps

  1. Open the model, Content › Changes.
  2. Check your changes.
  3. Commit with a message saying what and why.

If something goes wrong

A clash: choose yours or theirs for each, then Commit with these choices.
