See your background tasks

For: Practising architects

Long jobs you started (copies, loads, backups) and how far they are.

Why do this

Copies, loads and backups run in the background so you can carry on. Background tasks shows each one's progress and result.

EA Explorer covers a lot of ground. A few habits (search, help on every screen, your own requests and pins) make it feel smaller and get you to your work faster.

In TOGAF terms: General use of the tool, across every part of the Architecture Repository. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: You see how far your long jobs have got.

Steps

  1. Open Home › Requests › Background tasks.
  2. Read each task's progress; open it for its result.
  3. You are told when it ends.
