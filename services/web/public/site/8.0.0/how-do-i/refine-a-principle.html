Refine a principle

For: Project Admin, Practising architects

A principle that makes a broader one more specific.

Why do this

Broad principles sometimes need sharpening for a context: "Reuse before buy" might become "Reuse the group claims platform before buying". A refinement keeps the link to the broader rule. See Principles and standards.

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

In TOGAF terms: Architecture principles, refined for one architecture effort. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: A broad principle has a specific form for this project.

Before you start You need a role on the project (Architect or higher), unless a step says otherwise.

Steps

  1. Open the project, Principles.
  2. On the broader principle, Refine it.
  3. Write the statement; Save.
