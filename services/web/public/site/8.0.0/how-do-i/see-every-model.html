See every model and its state

For: Model Admin

Every model with its kind, level, owner and locks.

Why do this

Model Admins and System Admins need the whole list: kinds, levels, owners, locks. It is where to spot models nobody owns or that have gone stale. See Models: what they are and how they relate.

A model's life has stages: started, worked on, copied or split, handed over, locked or frozen at key moments, archived when done. Each stage has its wizard, and each asks before it changes anything.

In TOGAF terms: The Architecture Landscape in the Architecture Repository: the models at strategic, segment, capability and transition levels. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: You see every model and its state.

Steps

  1. Open Model Admin › Models.
  2. Filter by kind, level or state.
  3. Open a model's wizards from its row.
