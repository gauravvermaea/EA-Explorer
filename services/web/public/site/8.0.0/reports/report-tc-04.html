TC-04 Requirements catalogue

For: Practising architects, Executives, System Admin

Requirements with priority, what they realise and what realises them.

Why it matters

A catalogue, in the TOGAF Standard's content framework, is a structured list of one kind of building block with its key attributes: the reference others check against. This one belongs to the ADM phase shown above.

In TOGAF terms: A catalogue of the TOGAF content framework (phase: Requirements management). See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

Who it is for

Solution architect, Enterprise architect, Chief Compliance Officer, Internal audit.

How to use it

Use it as the TOGAF requirements catalogue: the list to review with its owners and keep current, filtered by model.

How to read it

Each row is one item with its key attributes; blanks are data to complete. Open goes to the item in EA Explorer.

Where to find it

Open a model, Reports › Reports, and choose Requirements catalogue; it opens its dashboard for that model. Every chart can be filtered, and most rows have Open to go to the entity in EA Explorer. The same data is in the model's OData feed for your own tools (OData feeds EA Explorer publishes).

For developers and report builders: it reads rpt.tc_requirements; its dashboard is eax-tc04.
