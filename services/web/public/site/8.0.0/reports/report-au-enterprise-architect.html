What it shows

What it gathers: MO Model overview, SR-07 Stakeholder and actor catalogue, SR-08 Motivation catalogue, SR-22 Impact analysis, SR-26 Completeness by type and owner, SR-30 Comparison with the source model, HM Pick-list heatmaps, VP-01 Organization view, VP-05 Layered view, VP-14 Stakeholder view, VP-15 Goal Realization view, VP-16 Requirements Realization view, VP-17 Motivation view, VP-18 Strategy view, VP-21 Outcome Realization view, VP-23 Project view, VP-24 Migration view, VP-25 Implementation and Migration view, VP-26 Gap view, VP-29 Driver/Goal/Outcome catalog view, VP-45 Business Footprint diagram view, VP-49 Business Scenario view, TC-01 Principles catalogue, TC-02 Stakeholder catalogue, TC-03 Driver, goal and outcome catalogue, TC-04 Requirements catalogue, TC-08 Work package and roadmap catalogue, TM-10 Stakeholder and concern matrix, TM-11 Plateau and architecture matrix, DR-01 Dependencies of an entity, DR-02 Dependency matrix between two types, DR-03 Impact chain from technology to value stream, GR-01 Gap analyses, GR-02 Gaps by domain and type, GR-03 TOGAF gap matrix, GR-04 Gap resolutions and work packages, GR-05 Gap entities in the models, QR-01 Data quality across models, QR-02 Completeness by type and owner, QR-03 What to fix, KR-01 KPI trends.

Why it matters

An audience dashboard gathers, on one page, the headline charts of the reports one kind of person usually needs, so they start from what matters to them instead of browsing the whole catalogue.

In TOGAF terms: Stakeholder management: each audience sees the views that address its concerns. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

Who it is for

Enterprise architect.

How to use it

Use it as the starting dashboard for the Enterprise architect dashboard: the reports that matter to this audience, together.

How to read it

Each chart is described where it comes from; open the full report from the Reports tab for detail.

Where to find it

Open a model, Reports › Reports, and choose Enterprise architect dashboard; it opens its dashboard for that model. Every chart can be filtered, and most rows have Open to go to the entity in EA Explorer. The same data is in the model's OData feed for your own tools (OData feeds EA Explorer publishes).
