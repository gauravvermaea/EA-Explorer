TM-09 Capability and organisation matrix

For: Practising architects, Executives

Which organisation units each capability depends on, through the applications, processes and roles that realise it.

On this page
  • Why it matters
  • Who it is for
  • How to use it
  • How to read it
  • Where to find it
  • Related

Why it matters

A matrix, in the TOGAF Standard's content framework, shows how two kinds of building block relate: rows of one kind, columns of the other, a mark where they are connected. It answers "what touches what" at a glance.

In TOGAF terms: A matrix of the TOGAF content framework (phase: B: business). See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

Who it is for

COO, Business unit head, Business architect.

How to use it

Use it as the TOGAF Capability and organisation matrix: which items of one kind relate to which of another.

How to read it

A filled cell is a relationship between the row and the column. Empty rows or columns are items with no link to the other kind; very full rows or columns are hubs.

Where to find it

Open a model, Reports › Reports, and choose Capability and organisation matrix; it opens its dashboard for that model. Every chart can be filtered, and most rows have Open to go to the entity in EA Explorer. The same data is in the model's OData feed for your own tools (OData feeds EA Explorer publishes).

For developers and report builders: it reads rpt.tm_capability_org; its dashboard is eax-tm09.
