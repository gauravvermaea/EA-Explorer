What it shows

What it gathers: SR-02 Business capability catalogue, SR-05 Business process catalogue, SR-10 Application against process, SR-13 Process against role, SR-14 Capability against value stream, SR-16 Capability heatmap, VP-01 Organization view, VP-10 Process Cooperation view, VP-18 Strategy view, VP-19 Capability Map view, VP-20 Value Stream view, VP-22 Resource Map view, VP-27 Organization/Actor catalog view, VP-28 Role catalog view, VP-31 Business Capabilities catalog view, VP-37 Business Interaction matrix view, VP-39 Value Stream/Capability matrix view, VP-41 Capability/Organization matrix view, VP-42 Organization Decomposition diagram view, TC-05 Location catalogue, TC-09 Role catalogue, TM-01 Business interaction matrix, TM-02 Actor and role matrix, TM-05 Location matrix, TM-08 Strategy and capability matrix, TM-09 Capability and organisation matrix.

Why it matters

An audience dashboard gathers, on one page, the headline charts of the reports one kind of person usually needs, so they start from what matters to them instead of browsing the whole catalogue.

In TOGAF terms: Stakeholder management: each audience sees the views that address its concerns. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

Who it is for

COO.

How to use it

Use it as the starting dashboard for the COO dashboard: the reports that matter to this audience, together.

How to read it

Each chart is described where it comes from; open the full report from the Reports tab for detail.

Where to find it

Open a model, Reports › Reports, and choose COO dashboard; it opens its dashboard for that model. Every chart can be filtered, and most rows have Open to go to the entity in EA Explorer. The same data is in the model's OData feed for your own tools (OData feeds EA Explorer publishes).
