Governance and sign-off

For: Executives, Project Admin, Practising architects

How decisions, exceptions and approvals are recorded, and why that record matters.

Why governance

Architecture is only useful if it is followed, and only followed if the decisions behind it are clear and fairly made. Governance is the practice of recording those decisions, checking work against them, and agreeing exceptions openly rather than letting them happen quietly. It protects the people making hard calls as much as the organisation.

What EA Explorer records

  • The governance log: decisions (with the options and reasons), dispensations (agreed exceptions, with an end date), compliance reviews and any records of your own kinds. See Log a governance record and Read the organisation's governance log.
  • Sign-off: each deliverable's approval or rejection, who decided, when, and the evidence (an email, the minutes). A project has sign-off people and a quorum. See Record a sign-off decision.
  • The architecture board: the group that accepts the organisation's records (See the architecture board).
  • History: every committed change to a model, by whom and why (See the change log).

For an executive, this is where approvals arrive and where the reasons behind a recommendation can be read.
