What it is made of

EA Explorer runs as a set of containers on one machine or several: the screens and the front door, the API and its job workers, the database, Keycloak for sign-in, reporting, documents, draw.io, the AI server and the operations helper. Everything is open source and runs on your own servers or laptop. See See the estate and See the containers.

Keeping it healthy

  • Health and alerts: a dot in the top bar, every part's state, checks every minute (Check the health and alerts).
  • Backups: taken on a schedule, checked by restoring (Take a backup and check it, Backups, restores and disaster recovery).
  • Secrets: every password and key a file, rotated when you choose (Rotate passwords and keys).
  • Updates: one release at a time, a pinned backup first, a roll back if needed (Update to a new release).
  • The self-test: the installation tests itself in its own fixtures (Run the self-test).

Anything that puts the maintenance page up warns first and waits for you to accept.
