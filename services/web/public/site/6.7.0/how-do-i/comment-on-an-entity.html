Comment on an entity

For: Practising architects

A comment others are told about; resolve it when done.

Why do this

Comments keep conversations beside the thing they are about, instead of in email. Those following the entity are told, and the comment is resolved when dealt with, so open questions are visible.

Content work is the everyday heart of architecture: recording what exists and what should, and how it all connects. EA Explorer checks it against the metamodel as you go and keeps it as your change until it is reviewed.

In TOGAF terms: Phases B, C and D: recording the building blocks and relationships of the baseline and target architectures. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: A question or note sits on the entity it is about.

Steps

  1. Open the entity; Comment.
  2. Write it; Comment. Those who follow it are told.
  3. Resolve it when it is dealt with.
