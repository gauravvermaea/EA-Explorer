See the licences

For: System Admin, Developers

The open-source parts EA Explorer uses and their licences.

Why do this

EA Explorer is free and open source, built from other open-source parts: the database, sign-in, reporting, documents, drawing. Legal and procurement teams often need to know each part and its licence before approving a tool; the list gives them that in one place.

The installation is built to look after itself as far as it can, and to ask before anything risky. These tasks are the decisions it leaves to you: what to run, when, and how far to trust a change.

In TOGAF terms: Outside the method: this keeps the tool that holds the Architecture Repository running. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: You see the open-source parts EA Explorer uses and their licences.

Steps

  1. Open System Admin › Settings › Licences.
  2. Read each part and its licence.
