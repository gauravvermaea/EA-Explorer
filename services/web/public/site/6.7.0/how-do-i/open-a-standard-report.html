Open a standard report

For: Practising architects, Executives

One of the 30 standard reports and dashboards.

Why do this

Standard reports answer common questions (application lifecycle, capability coverage, cost) on any model built on a matching metamodel, with no set-up. See Views, reports and KPIs.

Views and reports are where the model meets its audience. Choose the one that answers the question in front of you, for the person in front of you, and it stays current as the model changes.

In TOGAF terms: Architecture views and viewpoints: artifacts that address stakeholders' concerns, in any ADM phase. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: A ready-made report or dashboard on the model.

Steps

  1. Open the model, Reports › Reports.
  2. Open a report; it opens its dashboard for this model.
