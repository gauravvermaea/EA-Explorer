Say what a drawn shape is

For: Practising architects

Bind a shape on a drawn view to an entity, or make a new one.

Why do this

A drawn view is more than a picture when each shape is bound to an entity: then the drawing stays consistent with the model, clicking a shape opens the entity, and impact analysis sees what the drawing shows. See Views, reports and KPIs.

Views and reports are where the model meets its audience. Choose the one that answers the question in front of you, for the person in front of you, and it stays current as the model changes.

In TOGAF terms: Architecture views and viewpoints: artifacts that address stakeholders' concerns, in any ADM phase. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: A shape on a drawing is a real entity in the model.

Before you start You need a role on the model that may change content (Architect or higher).

Steps

  1. Drop a shape from the stencil onto the drawing.
  2. What is this?: search for the entity (near spellings work), or create a new one.
  3. Putting a shape inside another asks which relationship it means.
