Delete old change log entries

For: System Admin

Remove change log entries older than a period, for good.

Why do this

The change log grows with every commit. Removing entries older than a period keeps it manageable where your record-keeping rules allow. It cannot be undone, so check your organisation's retention needs first.

The installation is built to look after itself as far as it can, and to ask before anything risky. These tasks are the decisions it leaves to you: what to run, when, and how far to trust a change.

In TOGAF terms: Outside the method: this keeps the tool that holds the Architecture Repository running. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: The change log keeps only what you still need.

Before you start You need the Super Admin role.

Steps

  1. Open Architecture Work › Change log, Delete change log entries.
  2. Choose the period.
  3. Type to confirm; this cannot be undone.
