Track deliverables and sign-off

For: Project Admin, Practising architects

The project's documents and artifacts, their versions, and their approval by the sign-off people.

Why do this

Deliverables are what a project leaves behind; sign-off is the proof they were agreed. See Projects, deliverables and sign-off and Governance and sign-off.

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

In TOGAF terms: The ADM itself: the phases, steps, deliverables and sign-offs of an architecture project. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: Every deliverable is produced, versioned and signed off.

Before you start You need a role on the project.

The ways to do it

  1. The project's Deliverables and artifacts tab lists what the project produces; open one to see it, its versions and its sign-off.
  2. Documents live in the project's repository: check out, change, check in as a new version.
  3. The Sign-off tab lists what is waiting: the sign-off people approve, reject or record a decision with evidence, with a quorum where one is set.
  4. Decisions and dispensations go in the governance log.

Each task, one page

  1. Open a project deliverable
  2. Check a document out
  3. Check a document in
  4. Record a sign-off decision
  5. Send someone a sign-off link
  6. Log a governance record
  7. Finish a project
