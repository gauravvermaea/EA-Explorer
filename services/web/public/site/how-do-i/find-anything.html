Find anything

For: Users

Any function, model, project or entity, by name, ID, description or value.

Steps

  1. Press / or Ctrl+K anywhere, and type. It finds functions (every menu item, with where it is), the page's tabs and actions, help pages, and content (models, projects, entities) as you type. Near spellings work.
  2. For a search across every model you can read, with filters: Me › Search everything.
  3. Right-click any entity (a row, a card, a link) to open it, edit it, see its graph, history or related entities, or add it to a view.
