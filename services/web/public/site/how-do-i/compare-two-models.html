Compare two models or two moments

For: Users

What was added, changed and removed between two models, or one model at two tags.

Steps

  1. Architecture Work › Compare and analyse › Compare models.
  2. Choose two models, or one model at two tags.
  3. Read what was added, changed and removed, type by type; open any entity.
  4. Gap analysis (same menu) between a baseline and a target, with resolutions and work packages; Common analysis across many models.
