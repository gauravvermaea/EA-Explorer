Every task

Getting around

  • Find anythingAny function, model, project or entity, by name, ID, description or value.
  • Get help on any screenWhat the screen in front of you is for, and how to use it.

Working with content

  • Change content and commit itAdd and change entities and relationships, then commit them so everyone sees them.
  • Load data from ExcelDownload a sheet built for the model, fill it in, upload it and check the preview.

Views and reports

  • Compare two models or two momentsWhat was added, changed and removed between two models, or one model at two tags.
  • Draw a viewA view drawn in the built-in draw.io with the model's own entities, kept in step with the model.
  • Open a report or a viewThe standard views and reports, your own, and the model's KPIs.

Projects

  • Answer key questions with viewsThe questions stakeholders ask, answered with views and accepted by the stakeholder who asked.
  • Choose the project's ADM cycleA full ADM cycle, one of TOGAF's iterations, or your own set of phases.
  • Start an architecture projectA new project from one of the templates or blank, with its models, people and ADM cycle.
  • Track deliverables and sign-offThe project's documents and artifacts, their versions, and their approval by the sign-off people.

Models

  • Create a modelA new model: blank on a metamodel, a copy of another, a child that merges back, or started from a library model.

Metamodels

  • Build a metamodel in ExcelA whole metamodel as one workbook: types, attributes, allowed relationships and rules, made into a model.
  • Change a model's metamodelAdd or change entity types, attributes, pick lists, allowed relationships and rules, reviewed before they are published.

Data quality

  • Check and improve data qualitySee how complete, correct and current a model is, and fix what it finds.
  • Find and merge duplicatesEntities that are the same thing under close names, found and merged into one, their relationships moved to it.

Versions and merging

  • Merge a child model backA child's changes sent to its parent, reviewed change by change, and merged as one commit.
  • Tag a model and compareA named moment of a model to go back to, and what changed since.

Industry models

  • Publish a reference model to the libraryYour finished model in the model library, for everyone to start from.
  • Use an industry packStart from an industry's reference model, and download its book, workbook and templates.

Bringing data in

  • Push data with PythonA push source and the Python kit: your records sent in one call, proposed as a delivery.
  • Send data with a deliveryYour system writes the published Excel or CSV format; EA Explorer previews it for a Committer to accept.
  • Set up a delivery sourceAnother system sends its data to a model as deliveries, each previewed and accepted by a Committer.

Taking data out

  • Read a model in Power BI or ExcelThe model's OData feed: every entity type as a table, read only, with your permissions.
  • Receive webhooksSigned messages to your system when something happens in EA Explorer.

Access for systems

  • Call the REST APIEverything the screens do, from a script, described with OpenAPI.
  • Get a token or an API keyA credential for a script or another system, limited to what it needs.

People and access

  • Add a person and give them rolesPeople sign in through Keycloak; their roles on the installation come from Keycloak, and their roles on each model and project are given there.
  • Give people roles on a modelWho may read, change, commit and look after one model.
  • Map directory groups to rolesMake a Keycloak role or a directory group give an EA Explorer role, so people get their roles from the groups they are already in.

Security

  • Get an administrator back inA forgotten password, a locked account or a lost phone, sorted from the machine.
  • Limit who can reach EA ExplorerIP allow lists: only the addresses you list may reach EA Explorer, its sign-in pages, System Admin, the AI connector or the machine port.
  • Put in your organisation's certificateReplace the self-made certificate with your organisation's, so browsers trust EA Explorer without a warning.
  • Rotate passwords and keysChange EA Explorer's own passwords and keys, one at a time or all at once, now or on a schedule.
  • Turn on multi-factor sign-inAsk admins, or everyone, for a one-time code from an authenticator app when they sign in.

Backups and recovery

  • Back up a projectA project with its documents, models and library, as one file to keep or restore.
  • Restore a backupPut back a model, a project, part of the installation or all of it, from a backup.
  • Set up a DR siteA second EA Explorer kept up to date with this one, read only, ready to take over.
  • Take a backup and check itA backup of the whole installation now, and a check that it restores, without touching what is live.

Running it

  • Check the health and alertsSee whether every part of EA Explorer is up, what is wrong, and who is told.
  • Copy settings to another siteExport the admin settings to a file, and import them on another site, with every difference shown first.
  • Put up a bannerOne short message under the top bar for everyone, or for admins only, with optional start and end times.
  • Run the self-testCheck the installation works: the smoke test in a few minutes, or the full test.
  • Schedule a commandAn eax command that runs by itself: a nightly backup, a weekly check, or once at a time.
  • Update to a new releaseMove EA Explorer to a newer release, with a full backup first and a way back.

Surveys and maturity

  • Run a survey or a maturity assessmentQuestions to stakeholders through their task list, and EA capability maturity assessed and scored.

AI

  • Analyse with AIImpact, rationalisation, cost, technology risk and single points of failure, explained.
  • Change content with AIYour AI assistant adds and changes entities and relationships as your uncommitted changes.
  • Connect your AI assistantYour own AI app talks to EA Explorer through MCP, as you, with your permissions.
  • Discover the enterprise with AIYour AI assistant reads your sources and proposes what it finds for a model.
  • Explore a model with AIAsk your AI assistant about a model: what is in it, how things relate, what changed.
  • Look after the installation with AIFor admins: ask about the installation's state, usage and housekeeping.
  • Plan a project with AIAn ADM project planned from a template, scenarios compared, a change checked against principles.
  • Use AI for surveysA survey made from plain words, sent and summarised.
  • Write a report or a deck with AIA document or a presentation from a model or a project, with links to its sources.
