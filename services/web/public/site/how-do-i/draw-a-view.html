Draw a view

For: Users

A view drawn in the built-in draw.io with the model's own entities, kept in step with the model.

Before you start You need a role on the model; drawing relationships into the model needs Architect or higher.

Steps

  1. Open the model, its Views tab. Start a new drawn view (name it; it opens in its own tab), or open any picture (a network, a capability map, a standard view) and Save as a drawn view.
  2. Drag entities in from the search panel; the shapes use the ArchiMate 4 notation. A shape can be a placeholder until the entity exists.
  3. Lines you draw between entities go into the model as your change, only the relationship types the metamodel allows. Deleting a line asks: hide it in the view, or delete it from the model.
  4. Notes, comments and arrows from draw.io's general shapes never touch the model.
  5. Save: each save is kept as a version. Export as PNG, SVG, PDF or .drawio.
