Open a report or a view

For: Practising architects

The standard views and reports, your own, and the model's KPIs.

Why do this

A model answers questions through views, reports and KPIs. This guide shows which to use when. See Views, reports and KPIs.

Views and reports are where the model meets its audience. Choose the one that answers the question in front of you, for the person in front of you, and it stays current as the model changes.

In TOGAF terms: Architecture views and viewpoints: artifacts that address stakeholders' concerns, in any ADM phase. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: You get the view, report or KPI that answers your question.

The ways to do it

  1. Open the model, its Views tab: the 49 standard views, each as a diagram, catalogue, heat map, matrix and relationship list, and the drawn views.
  2. Its Reports tab: the standard reports and dashboards, the Report designer for your own (a type, its columns, related types and filters), and the OData address for Power BI and Excel.
  3. Its KPIs tab: the model's key figures month by month and at every tag.
  4. Click any box in a picture to open the entity.
