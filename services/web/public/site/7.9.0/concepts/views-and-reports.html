A view answers one question for one audience

A model can hold thousands of entities; nobody reads it whole. A view selects what one audience needs to see to answer one question, and shows it the way they read best: a diagram for structure, a catalogue for a list, a matrix for "what touches what", a heat map for "where is it worst". Because views read the model, they are always as current as the model and never contradict each other.

What EA Explorer offers

  • 49 standard views in five forms each: diagram, catalogue, heat map, matrix and relationships (Open a standard view). Copy one to make it your own (Copy a view into your model).
  • Drawn views: diagrams you lay out by hand in draw.io, each shape bound to a real entity (Draw a view).
  • Graphs: an entity's network and its impact, many steps out (See the impact of a change).
  • Reports and dashboards: 30 standard ones, and your own from the report designer (Build your own report).
  • KPIs: the model's key figures, kept every month and at every tag, to show the trend (See a model's KPIs).
  • Outside tools: Power BI or Excel read the model live (Read a model in Power BI).

For an executive, the dashboards and KPIs give the state of the estate at a glance, and a project's report gives where each deliverable stands.
