Your role

Choose what you do with EA Explorer: each page says what the platform has for you, how it is put together, and the tasks you will do.

  • System AdminRun the installation: people, security, backups, updates, health
  • Model AdminLook after models and their metamodels: people, quality, versions, merges
  • Project AdminRun architecture projects: templates, phases, deliverables, sign-off
  • Practising architectsFind, change, draw, report and commit the architecture
  • Industry model makersBuild reference models and metamodels for an industry
  • DevelopersBring data in and take it out: the API, feeds, formats, scripts, AI
  • ExecutivesSee where the architecture and its projects stand, and approve what needs you
  • Platform developersChange EA Explorer itself: run it locally, debug, test, release
