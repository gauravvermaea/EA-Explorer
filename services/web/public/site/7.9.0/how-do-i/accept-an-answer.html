Accept an answer to your question

For: Practising architects, Executives

As the stakeholder: accept the answer, or say what is missing.

Why do this

A key question is something a stakeholder needs to know for the project to serve them. When the architects answer it, the person who asked is the right judge of whether it is answered. Accepting closes the loop; sending it back keeps the project honest. See Stakeholders and key questions.

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

In TOGAF terms: Stakeholder management: confirming a concern is addressed. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: Your question is closed, or sent back with what is missing.

Steps

  1. Open the task you were sent (or the project's Key questions).
  2. Read the answer and its views.
  3. Accept, or send it back saying what is missing.
