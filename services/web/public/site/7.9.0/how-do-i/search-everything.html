Why do this

Search looks across everything you have access to at once, so you do not need to know which model or project holds what you are looking for.

EA Explorer covers a lot of ground. A few habits (search, help on every screen, your own requests and pins) make it feel smaller and get you to your work faster.

In TOGAF terms: General use of the tool, across every part of the Architecture Repository. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: You find any model, entity, project or document you may see.

Steps

  1. Open Home › Search everything (or press / and choose search).
  2. Type the words; filter by kind or model.
  3. Open a result.
