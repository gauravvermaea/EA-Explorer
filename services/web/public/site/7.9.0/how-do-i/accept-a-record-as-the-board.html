Accept a record as the architecture board

For: Executives

Accept or reject a decision, dispensation or review sent to the board.

Why do this

The architecture board is where the organisation's significant decisions and exceptions are agreed: a choice between options, a dispensation from a standard, the outcome of a compliance review. Recording its acceptance turns a team's proposal into an organisational decision that others can rely on and point to. See Governance and sign-off.

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

In TOGAF terms: Architecture governance: the architecture board. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: The board's decision on a record is made and kept.

Before you start You need the Architecture Board role.

Steps

  1. Open Repository & Content Framework › Architecture board.
  2. Open a record waiting: what was decided, the options, the evidence.
  3. Accept, or reject saying why.
