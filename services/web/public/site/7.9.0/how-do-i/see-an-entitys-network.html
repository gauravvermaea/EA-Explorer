See an entity's network

For: Practising architects

Everything around an entity, one to three steps out.

Why do this

The network shows an entity with everything related to it, one to three steps out. It is the quickest way to understand an unfamiliar part of the model.

Views and reports are where the model meets its audience. Choose the one that answers the question in front of you, for the person in front of you, and it stays current as the model changes.

In TOGAF terms: Architecture views and viewpoints: artifacts that address stakeholders' concerns, in any ADM phase. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: You see everything around an entity.

Steps

  1. Open the model, Content › Graph (or right-click an entity, Graph).
  2. Choose the entity and how many steps.
  3. Click any box to open the entity.
