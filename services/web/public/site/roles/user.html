What EA Explorer has for users

You find, change, draw and report on the architecture: entities and relationships, views, reports, projects, and your own AI assistant.

Who this is for

Everyone who works with the architecture day to day: Architects (change content), Committers (review and commit changes), History Viewers and Report Viewers (read and report), and the people on projects (Architect, Stakeholder). What you may do depends on your roles on each model and project: what you may not use is not shown.

How it is put together

  • Entities and relationships: every element of the architecture (a capability, a process, an application, a server...) is an entity of a type; relationships join them, only in the ways the metamodel allows. Each entity has an ID, attributes, links to documents, a history and the views it appears in.
  • Your changes are private until committed: what you add or change is your uncommitted change, seen by you and the model's Committers, until it is committed. A Committer reviews and commits it, or sends it back.
  • Views are drawn from the repository when you open them, so they always show the data as it is now. A drawn view you make in draw.io stays in step with the model: a relationship you draw goes into the model as your change.
  • History: every change is kept, so you can see any entity or model as it was at any moment or tag.

How do I: your tasks

Getting around

  • Find anythingAny function, model, project or entity, by name, ID, description or value.
  • Get help on any screenWhat the screen in front of you is for, and how to use it.

Working with content

  • Change content and commit itAdd and change entities and relationships, then commit them so everyone sees them.
  • Load data from ExcelDownload a sheet built for the model, fill it in, upload it and check the preview.

Views and reports

  • Compare two models or two momentsWhat was added, changed and removed between two models, or one model at two tags.
  • Draw a viewA view drawn in the built-in draw.io with the model's own entities, kept in step with the model.
  • Open a report or a viewThe standard views and reports, your own, and the model's KPIs.

Projects

  • Answer key questions with viewsThe questions stakeholders ask, answered with views and accepted by the stakeholder who asked.
  • Track deliverables and sign-offThe project's documents and artifacts, their versions, and their approval by the sign-off people.

Data quality

  • Check and improve data qualitySee how complete, correct and current a model is, and fix what it finds.
  • Find and merge duplicatesEntities that are the same thing under close names, found and merged into one, their relationships moved to it.

Versions and merging

  • Tag a model and compareA named moment of a model to go back to, and what changed since.

Industry models

  • Use an industry packStart from an industry's reference model, and download its book, workbook and templates.

Taking data out

  • Read a model in Power BI or ExcelThe model's OData feed: every entity type as a table, read only, with your permissions.

AI

  • Analyse with AIImpact, rationalisation, cost, technology risk and single points of failure, explained.
  • Change content with AIYour AI assistant adds and changes entities and relationships as your uncommitted changes.
  • Connect your AI assistantYour own AI app talks to EA Explorer through MCP, as you, with your permissions.
  • Discover the enterprise with AIYour AI assistant reads your sources and proposes what it finds for a model.
  • Explore a model with AIAsk your AI assistant about a model: what is in it, how things relate, what changed.
  • Write a report or a deck with AIA document or a presentation from a model or a project, with links to its sources.
