What EA Explorer has for Project Admins

You run architecture projects: start them from a template, choose the ADM cycle, look after the people, deliverables, key questions and sign-off.

Who this is for

People with the Project Admin or Lead Architect role on a project. Project Admins look after the projects, templates and libraries of the installation under the Project Admin menu.

How it is put together

  • A project runs one ADM cycle (or a piece of architecture work) with its own people, models, deliverables and documents. It starts from one of 14 templates (for example transformation, as-is documentation, build or buy, cloud migration, acquisition) or blank.
  • The TOGAF 10 library gives the phases, steps and deliverables: 13 phases, 116 steps, 171 deliverables and artifacts, and 51 repository folders. You add your own steps and order them.
  • The ADM cycle is a Full cycle, one of TOGAF's iterations (Architecture Capability, Development, Transition Planning, Governance) or your own set of phases. Phases left out are frozen, never deleted.
  • Models in roles: a project uses models as its baseline, target, transition, scenario, reference or working copy, each from a tag of the model it starts from.
  • Documents live in the project's repository (Mayan EDMS): versions, check-out, acceptance and approval.
  • Sign-off is by the project's sign-off people, with a quorum; decisions and dispensations go in the governance log.
  • Project roles: Project Admin, Lead Architect, Architect, Model Admin, Model Sub Admin and Stakeholder.

How do I: your tasks

Getting around

  • Get help on any screenWhat the screen in front of you is for, and how to use it.

Projects

  • Answer key questions with viewsThe questions stakeholders ask, answered with views and accepted by the stakeholder who asked.
  • Choose the project's ADM cycleA full ADM cycle, one of TOGAF's iterations, or your own set of phases.
  • Start an architecture projectA new project from one of the templates or blank, with its models, people and ADM cycle.
  • Track deliverables and sign-offThe project's documents and artifacts, their versions, and their approval by the sign-off people.

Backups and recovery

  • Back up a projectA project with its documents, models and library, as one file to keep or restore.
  • Restore a backupPut back a model, a project, part of the installation or all of it, from a backup.

Surveys and maturity

  • Run a survey or a maturity assessmentQuestions to stakeholders through their task list, and EA capability maturity assessed and scored.

AI

  • Plan a project with AIAn ADM project planned from a template, scenarios compared, a change checked against principles.
  • Use AI for surveysA survey made from plain words, sent and summarised.
  • Write a report or a deck with AIA document or a presentation from a model or a project, with links to its sources.
