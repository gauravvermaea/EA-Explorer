Compare business scenarios

For: Practising architects, Project Admin

Options for the future, side by side, in a project with a scenario model.

Why do this

Scenario planning describes two or more possible targets from the same starting point. Comparing them shows what each changes, costs and risks, so the choice is made on evidence. See Kinds of architecture project and their goals.

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

In TOGAF terms: Business scenarios and option analysis, used in phases A, B and E. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: Possible futures are compared side by side before one is chosen.

Steps

  1. Open the project, Architecture › Scenarios.
  2. Pick the scenarios to compare.
  3. Read the differences and their scores.
