Read a dashboard

For: Executives, Practising architects

The state of a model's estate at a glance: counts, health, cost, lifecycle.

Why do this

Dashboards summarise a model for people who need the picture, not the detail: how many applications, in what health, at what cost, nearing end of life. A click on a chart shows what is behind it. See Views, reports and KPIs.

Views and reports are where the model meets its audience. Choose the one that answers the question in front of you, for the person in front of you, and it stays current as the model changes.

In TOGAF terms: Architecture views and viewpoints: artifacts that address stakeholders' concerns, in any ADM phase. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: The state of the estate at a glance.

Steps

  1. Open the model, Reports.
  2. Open a dashboard.
  3. Filter it; click a chart to see what is behind it.
