Send someone a sign-off link

For: Project Admin, Practising architects, Executives

A link a stakeholder opens to sign off.

Why do this

Approvers are busy and rarely browse a project to find what waits for them. A direct link, pasted into your email, takes them straight to the right deliverable's sign-off after they sign in, which shortens the wait for approvals.

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

In TOGAF terms: Architecture governance: approval of deliverables. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: An approver gets a link straight to what they must approve.

Steps

  1. Open the deliverable's sign-off.
  2. Link to this sign-off: copy it.
  3. Send it; they open it after signing in.
