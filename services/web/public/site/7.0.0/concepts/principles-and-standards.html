Principles and standards

For: Project Admin, Practising architects, Executives

The rules architecture decisions follow, why they are written down, and how projects use them.

Why principles

A principle is a short, lasting rule that guides decisions: "Data is an asset", "Buy before build", "Reuse before buy". Each has a statement, the reasoning behind it, and what it implies in practice. Written down and agreed, they let many people make consistent decisions without asking the same questions every time, and they give a fair basis for saying no.

Standards are more specific: the technologies, products, formats and practices the organisation has chosen.

How EA Explorer uses them

The organisation's principles live in a library (Look after the principles library); each project picks the ones it follows and may add or refine its own (Add a principle to a project, Refine a principle). Standards are added the same way (Add a standard a project follows). Compliance reviews check a solution against both (Run a compliance review), and an agreed exception is recorded as a dispensation in the governance log. See Governance and sign-off.
