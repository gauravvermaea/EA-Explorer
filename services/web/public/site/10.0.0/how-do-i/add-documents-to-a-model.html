Add documents to a model

For: Practising architects

Files kept with a model and linked to its entities.

Why do this

Some knowledge belongs in documents rather than entities: a design rationale, a vendor contract, workshop notes. Keeping them with the model, classified and linked to entities, means they are found where they are relevant instead of on someone's drive.

Content work is the everyday heart of architecture: recording what exists and what should, and how it all connects. EA Explorer checks it against the metamodel as you go and keeps it as your change until it is reviewed.

In TOGAF terms: Phases B, C and D: recording the building blocks and relationships of the baseline and target architectures. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: Documents that explain a model are kept with it.

Before you start You need a role on the model that may change content (Architect or higher).

Steps

  1. Open the model, Documents.
  2. Add a document: the file, its class and title; Save.
  3. Link it from an entity.
