Detach a model from its parent

For: Model Admin

Break the link: it no longer merges back or takes the parent's changes.

Why do this

A child model merges back into its parent and takes its changes. When that link no longer makes sense (the work went its own way, the parent is retired), detaching makes it an independent model. See Models: what they are and how they relate.

A model's life has stages: started, worked on, copied or split, handed over, locked or frozen at key moments, archived when done. Each stage has its wizard, and each asks before it changes anything.

In TOGAF terms: The Architecture Landscape in the Architecture Repository: the models at strategic, segment, capability and transition levels. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: A child model stands on its own.

Before you start You need the Model Admin role on the model.

Steps

  1. Open Model Admin › Wizards, Detach or reparent, and pick the child.
  2. Choose Detach; give a reason.
  3. Confirm.
