Map a customer journey

For: Practising architects, Project Admin

Stages, touchpoints and pain points of a customer's journey.

Why do this

A customer journey maps the stages a customer goes through, the touchpoints at each, and where it hurts. It grounds the architecture in what customers experience and shows which capabilities and systems matter most.

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

In TOGAF terms: Phase B, Business Architecture: how customers experience the business. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: You see the change through the customer's eyes.

Before you start You need a role on the project (Architect or higher).

Steps

  1. Open the project, Business › Customer journeys.
  2. Add a journey; add its stages and touchpoints.
  3. Mark the pain points; Save.
