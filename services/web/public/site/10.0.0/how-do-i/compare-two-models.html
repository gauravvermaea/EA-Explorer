Compare two models or two moments

For: Practising architects

What was added, changed and removed between two models, or one model at two tags.

Why do this

Comparison is the basis of gap analysis, of checking a merge, and of explaining change: what was added, changed and removed, type by type. Comparing a model with one of its own tags shows how it moved over time. See Versions, tags and merging.

Views and reports are where the model meets its audience. Choose the one that answers the question in front of you, for the person in front of you, and it stays current as the model changes.

In TOGAF terms: Architecture views and viewpoints: artifacts that address stakeholders' concerns, in any ADM phase. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: You see exactly what differs between two models, or one model at two moments.

Steps

  1. Architecture Work › Compare and analyse › Compare models.
  2. Choose two models, or one model at two tags.
  3. Read what was added, changed and removed, type by type; open any entity.
