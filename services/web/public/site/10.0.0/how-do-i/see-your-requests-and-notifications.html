See requests and notifications

For: Practising architects

What people asked of you, and what you are told.

Why do this

Work in EA Explorer reaches you from many places: a sign-off to give, a change sent back, a delivery to review, a question answered. Notifications gather all of them in one place, with requests that need you kept apart from news, so nothing addressed to you is missed.

EA Explorer covers a lot of ground. A few habits (search, help on every screen, your own requests and pins) make it feel smaller and get you to your work faster.

In TOGAF terms: General use of the tool, across every part of the Architecture Repository. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: You see what people asked of you and what you are told.

Steps

  1. Open Home › Notifications.
  2. Open Requests to me for what needs you.
  3. Mark them done as you go.
