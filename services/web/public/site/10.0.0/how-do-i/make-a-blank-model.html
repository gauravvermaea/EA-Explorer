Make a blank model

For: Model Admin, Industry model makers

A new, empty model on the metamodel you choose.

On this page
  • Why do this
  • Steps
  • Check it worked
  • Related

Why do this

A blank model is right when nothing existing fits: a new area of the business, a new kind of analysis. Choosing the metamodel it starts from decides what it can hold. See Models: what they are and how they relate and Metamodels: the shape of a model.

A model's life has stages: started, worked on, copied or split, handed over, locked or frozen at key moments, archived when done. Each stage has its wizard, and each asks before it changes anything.

In TOGAF terms: The Architecture Landscape in the Architecture Repository: the models at strategic, segment, capability and transition levels. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: A new, empty model on the metamodel you choose.

Before you start You need the Model Creator role.

Steps

  1. Open Model Admin › Wizards and choose New model.
  2. Name it and choose what it starts from: ArchiMate 4, the TOGAF 10 content metamodel, an industry's metamodel, or your own.
  3. Create the model.
  4. Open it and give people their roles on its People tab.

Check it worked

It is listed on Model Admin › Models.
