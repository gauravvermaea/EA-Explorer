Fill in the business model canvas

For: Practising architects, Project Admin

The nine boxes of the business the project serves.

Why do this

The business model canvas describes how an organisation creates and delivers value in nine boxes: customers, value propositions, channels, relationships, revenue, resources, activities, partners and costs. It anchors the business architecture in what the business is actually for. See The ADM: how architecture work is done.

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

In TOGAF terms: Phase B, Business Architecture: the business the architecture serves. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: The business the architecture serves is described on one page.

Before you start You need a role on the project (Architect or higher).

Steps

  1. Open the project, Business › Business model canvas.
  2. Fill in each box.
  3. Save; print it for workshops.
