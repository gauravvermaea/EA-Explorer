Choose the shapes on the stencil

For: Practising architects

Show or hide groups of shapes and their size.

Why do this

The stencil holds the shapes for each domain of the metamodel. Showing only the groups you need makes drawing faster and keeps views to one purpose. See Views, reports and KPIs.

Views and reports are where the model meets its audience. Choose the one that answers the question in front of you, for the person in front of you, and it stays current as the model changes.

In TOGAF terms: Architecture views and viewpoints: artifacts that address stakeholders' concerns, in any ADM phase. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: The shapes you need are at hand when you draw.

Steps

  1. Open a drawn view; The stencil.
  2. Show or hide each domain's group; choose the size.
