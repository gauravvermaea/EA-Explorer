Backups, restores and disaster recovery

For: System Admin, Model Admin, Project Admin

What is backed up, the kinds of restore from one model to everything, and how a DR site keeps you running.

On this page
  • Why it matters
  • The layers
  • Read next

Why it matters

The repository holds years of an organisation's thinking. Losing it, or a part of it, costs far more than the effort of protecting it. A backup that has never been restored is a hope, not a backup; so EA Explorer restores its own backups to check them.

The layers

  • Tags and history put back one model, or one entity, without any backup at all (Put a model back to a tag).
  • Backups of the databases, files and settings, on a schedule, pinned when they matter (Set when backups are taken).
  • Restores from one model or project up to everything; a backup of now is always taken first (Restore a backup).
  • A DR pack and a DR site: everything needed to rebuild elsewhere, and a second site kept up to date that can take over (Make a DR pack, Set up a DR site).
