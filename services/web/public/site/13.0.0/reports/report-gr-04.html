GR-04 Gap resolutions and work packages

For: Practising architects, Executives

How each gap is closed (build, buy, retire, replace...) and the work packages that close it; the gaps still undecided.

On this page
  • Why it matters
  • Who it is for
  • How to use it
  • How to read it
  • Where to find it
  • Related

Why it matters

Gap analysis compares a baseline with a target and keeps the differences: the bridge, in ADM phase E, from the target architecture to the plan.

In TOGAF terms: ADM phase E, Opportunities and Solutions (gap analysis is also used in phases B to D). See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

Who it is for

Portfolio manager, Change manager, Enterprise architect, CIO.

How to use it

Use it to follow the decisions on gaps: resolution and work packages.

How to read it

Gaps without a resolution are undecided; gaps with a resolution but no work package are decided but not planned. Both should be empty before the roadmap is final.

Where to find it

Open a model, Reports › Reports, and choose Gap resolutions and work packages; it opens its dashboard for that model. Every chart can be filtered, and most rows have Open to go to the entity in EA Explorer. The same data is in the model's OData feed for your own tools (OData feeds EA Explorer publishes).

For developers and report builders: it reads rpt.gap_items; its dashboard is eax-gr04.
