Write a report or a deck with AI

For: Practising architects, Project Admin

A document or a presentation from a model or a project, with links to its sources.

Why do this

Reports for stakeholders take time to write. Your AI assistant can draft one from the model's content, with the entities it used, for you to check and finish. See AI in EA Explorer.

Using AI in EA Explorer is always your assistant working with your rights: it reads and drafts, you review and commit. Used well, it takes the slow parts of architecture work (searching, cross-checking, first drafts) so you spend your time on judgement.

In TOGAF terms: Outside the standard's method, but in service of it: AI speeds up gathering content (phases B to D), analysis (E) and reporting to stakeholders, with people reviewing every change. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: Your AI assistant drafts a report from the model.

Before you start Connect your AI assistant.

Things to ask

  • Write a current-state summary of the claims domain as a Word document in the project.
  • Write an executive summary of this gap analysis.
  • Make a ten-slide deck on this project: its outline, key views and decisions.

The document is saved in the project for review; a deck's views come as pictures or data from EA Explorer.
