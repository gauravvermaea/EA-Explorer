Pin a model or project

For: Practising architects

Keep the ones you use at the top of your lists and the menu.

Why do this

Pinning keeps the models and projects you work on at the top of your lists and the menu, so you go straight to your work.

EA Explorer covers a lot of ground. A few habits (search, help on every screen, your own requests and pins) make it feel smaller and get you to your work faster.

In TOGAF terms: General use of the tool, across every part of the Architecture Repository. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: What you use most is always at hand.

Steps

  1. Open Architecture Work › Recent and pinned.
  2. Click the pin beside a model or project.
  3. It stays at the top until you unpin it.
