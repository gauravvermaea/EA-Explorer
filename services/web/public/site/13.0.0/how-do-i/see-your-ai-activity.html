See what your AI assistant did

For: Practising architects, System Admin

Every call your AI assistant made in your name.

Why do this

Every call your AI assistant makes is recorded: the tool, what it read, what it changed. You can check its work and undo anything it changed. See AI in EA Explorer.

Using AI in EA Explorer is always your assistant working with your rights: it reads and drafts, you review and commit. Used well, it takes the slow parts of architecture work (searching, cross-checking, first drafts) so you spend your time on judgement.

In TOGAF terms: Outside the standard's method, but in service of it: AI speeds up gathering content (phases B to D), analysis (E) and reporting to stakeholders, with people reviewing every change. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: You see what your AI assistant did in your name.

Steps

  1. Open AI › My AI activity.
  2. Read each call: the tool, what it read or changed.
  3. Anything it changed is an uncommitted change you can undo.
