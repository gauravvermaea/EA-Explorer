Edit entities in the grid

For: Practising architects

Change many entities at once in a table, like a spreadsheet.

Why do this

The grid is the fastest way to work on many entities of one type: fill in a column, fix values, paste from Excel. It still checks the metamodel and quality rules as you go, and changes are uncommitted until committed.

Content work is the everyday heart of architecture: recording what exists and what should, and how it all connects. EA Explorer checks it against the metamodel as you go and keeps it as your change until it is reviewed.

In TOGAF terms: Phases B, C and D: recording the building blocks and relationships of the baseline and target architectures. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: Many entities are changed quickly, like a spreadsheet.

Before you start You need a role on the model that may change content (Architect or higher).

Steps

  1. Open the model, Content › Grid, and choose the entity type.
  2. Click a cell to edit; copy and paste from Excel with Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V.
  3. Filter or sort a column from its header.
  4. Your changes are uncommitted until committed.
