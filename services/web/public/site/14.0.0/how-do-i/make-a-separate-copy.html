Make a separate copy of a model

For: Model Admin

A copy on its own: it never merges back, but can still be compared.

On this page
  • Why do this
  • Steps
  • Related

Why do this

A separate copy has no link to its original: it never merges back and does not take its changes. It suits experiments, training, or a model for another purpose. See Models: what they are and how they relate.

A model's life has stages: started, worked on, copied or split, handed over, locked or frozen at key moments, archived when done. Each stage has its wizard, and each asks before it changes anything.

In TOGAF terms: The Architecture Landscape in the Architecture Repository: the models at strategic, segment, capability and transition levels. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: A copy of a model that goes its own way.

Before you start You need the Model Admin role on the model.

Steps

  1. Open Model Admin › Wizards on the model.
  2. Choose Copy without the link.
  3. Name the copy; with or without the data; Make a copy now.
