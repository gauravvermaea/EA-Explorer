Open a project deliverable

For: Practising architects, Project Admin

What a deliverable is, its versions, evidence and sign-off.

Why do this

A deliverable is what a project phase produces. Opening it shows the document, its versions, the evidence linked to it, and who has signed it off. See Projects, deliverables and sign-off.

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

In TOGAF terms: The ADM itself: the phases, steps, deliverables and sign-offs of an architecture project. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: You see a deliverable, its versions and where its sign-off stands.

Steps

  1. Open the project, Plan › Phases, and the phase.
  2. Open the deliverable.
  3. Read its versions, evidence and where its sign-off stands.
