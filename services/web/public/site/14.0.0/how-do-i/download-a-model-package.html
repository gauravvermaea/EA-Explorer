Download a model as a package

For: Model Admin, Developers

One file of the whole model, to keep, move or put on git.

On this page
  • Why do this
  • Steps
  • Related

Why do this

A model package holds everything of one model, with or without data, optionally with its history. It is how a model moves between installations or into git, and a simple way to keep a copy before major change.

A model's life has stages: started, worked on, copied or split, handed over, locked or frozen at key moments, archived when done. Each stage has its wizard, and each asks before it changes anything.

In TOGAF terms: The Architecture Landscape in the Architecture Repository: the models at strategic, segment, capability and transition levels. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: A whole model is in one file, to keep or move.

Before you start You need a role on the model.

Steps

  1. Open Model Admin › Wizards on the model and choose Download.
  2. Choose Excel or zip, and without data, with data, or deep with its history.
  3. Download, or Put it on git.
