Bring data in with the guide

For: Practising architects, Developers

A step-by-step guide whichever way the data comes.

Why do this

There are several ways to bring data into a model: a sheet, a folder another system drops into, a feed, a script, documents read by AI. The guide asks where your data is and walks you through the way that fits. See Data in and out: integration.

Every route in ends the same way: what arrives is shown as new, changed, kept and problem rows, and a person decides. That review is what lets you automate feeds without losing control of the model.

In TOGAF terms: Supports the content of phases B, C and D (baseline architectures), keeping the Architecture Landscape current from the systems that already know it. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: The right way to bring your data in, step by step.

Steps

  1. Open the model, Import and export, Bring data in.
  2. Choose where the data is: a file, a folder another system drops into, an OData feed, a system that sends it, or documents read by AI.
  3. Follow the steps; what arrives waits for review.
