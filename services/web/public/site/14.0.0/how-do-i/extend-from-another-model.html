Extend a model from another

For: Model Admin, Industry model makers

Take types and relationships from another model's metamodel.

Why do this

Rather than designing types from scratch, you can take them from a model that already has them, such as a well-tuned industry model. The changes come as a draft for review. See Metamodels: the shape of a model.

A metamodel change ripples: into what people may record, what reports read, what rules check and what integrations send. That is why it goes through drafts and review, and why each version is kept and can be compared.

In TOGAF terms: The content framework and content metamodel: what building blocks and relationships the architecture may hold. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: Your model's metamodel takes types and relationships another model already has.

Before you start You need the Model Admin role on the model.

Steps

  1. Open Model Admin › Wizards, Extend from another model.
  2. Pick the source model and what to take.
  3. A draft is made for review.
