Read a discovery run's report

For: Model Admin

What a run found, skipped, doubted and no longer found.

Why do this

Each discovery run reports what it found and where, what it skipped (titles only, for documents marked NOT FOR AI), what may be duplicates, and what is no longer found. You accept or reject each proposal. See AI in EA Explorer.

Using AI in EA Explorer is always your assistant working with your rights: it reads and drafts, you review and commit. Used well, it takes the slow parts of architecture work (searching, cross-checking, first drafts) so you spend your time on judgement.

In TOGAF terms: Outside the standard's method, but in service of it: AI speeds up gathering content (phases B to D), analysis (E) and reporting to stakeholders, with people reviewing every change. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: You see what the AI found, skipped and doubted, and decide.

Before you start You need the Discovery Admin role.

Steps

  1. Open the discovery project and a run.
  2. Read Found, each with where it was found; documents marked NOT FOR AI were skipped by title only.
  3. Accept or reject what it proposes.
