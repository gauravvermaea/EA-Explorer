AU-it_operations IT operations dashboard

For: Practising architects, System Admin

The headline view of every report a it operations usually wants, on one page.

What it shows

What it gathers: SR-03 Technology component inventory, SR-06 Interface and integration catalogue, SR-17 Technology lifecycle and end of life, SR-24 Single points of failure, VP-04 Technology view, VP-06 Physical view, VP-09 Technology Usage view, VP-13 Implementation and Deployment view, TC-05 Location catalogue, TM-05 Location matrix, DR-01 Dependencies of an entity, DR-04 Dependency counts and hot spots.

Why it matters

An audience dashboard gathers, on one page, the headline charts of the reports one kind of person usually needs, so they start from what matters to them instead of browsing the whole catalogue.

In TOGAF terms: Stakeholder management: each audience sees the views that address its concerns. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

Who it is for

IT operations.

How to use it

Use it as the starting dashboard for the IT operations dashboard: the reports that matter to this audience, together.

How to read it

Each chart is described where it comes from; open the full report from the Reports tab for detail.

Where to find it

Open a model, Reports › Reports, and choose IT operations dashboard; it opens its dashboard for that model. Every chart can be filtered, and most rows have Open to go to the entity in EA Explorer. The same data is in the model's OData feed for your own tools (OData feeds EA Explorer publishes).
