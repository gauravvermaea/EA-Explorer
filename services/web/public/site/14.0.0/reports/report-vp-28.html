VP-28 Role catalog view

For: Practising architects, Executives

The model through the Role catalog view: the roles in the business and their responsibilities. As a catalog, a matrix, a network and a heat map. EA Explorer's own view.

What it shows

Its concerns: The roles in the business and their responsibilities. For: Business Architects, HR, managers. Purpose: Informing.

Element types shown: Role.

Relationships shown: Composition, Aggregation, Assignment, Realization, Serving, Access, Influence, Association, Triggering, Flow, Specialization. The heat map colours by Owner.

The TOGAF artifact it supports: Role catalog.

Reports that go with it: TC-09.

Why it matters

Views from the ArchiMate 4 Specification each serve a set of stakeholders and concerns, by showing only the element and relationship types that matter to them. EA Explorer shows each one from your model as a catalogue, a matrix, a network and a heat map.

In TOGAF terms: An architecture view, addressing stakeholders' concerns; it usually supports the TOGAF artifact named below. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

Who it is for

COO, Business architect.

How to use it

Use it to look at the model the way the Role catalog view's stakeholders need: only the element and relationship types that matter to their concerns.

How to read it

The catalog lists the elements, the relationships table shows how they connect, and the heat map colours elements by the view's attribute. Missing elements of a type mean that part is not modelled yet.

Where to find it

Open a model, Reports › Reports, and choose Role catalog view; it opens its dashboard for that model. Every chart can be filtered, and most rows have Open to go to the entity in EA Explorer. The same data is in the model's OData feed for your own tools (OData feeds EA Explorer publishes).

For developers and report builders: it reads rpt.vp_elements; its dashboard is eax-vp28.
