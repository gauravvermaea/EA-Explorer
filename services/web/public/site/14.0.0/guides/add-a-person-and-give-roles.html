Add a person and give them roles

For: System Admin

People sign in through Keycloak; their roles on the installation come from Keycloak, and their roles on each model and project are given there.

Why do this

Access in EA Explorer comes in layers: signing in at all (Keycloak or your directory), roles across the installation, and roles on each model and project. A new person usually needs all three, in that order. See Roles and access.

Access in EA Explorer is layered so each owner decides for their own part: System Admins for the installation, Model Admins for models, Project Admins for projects. Most people need only a few roles to do their work.

In TOGAF terms: The Preliminary phase's architecture capability: who does architecture work and with what authority. Sign-in itself is outside the method. See EA Explorer and the TOGAF Standard.

The goal: A new colleague can sign in and work on the right models and projects.

Before you start You need the Keycloak Admin or User Admin role to add people, and Model Admin or Project Admin on the model or project you give roles on.

The ways to do it

  1. Add the person in Keycloak (or let them come from your directory, when EA Explorer signs in against it). Open https://<your EA Explorer address>/auth/admin/, choose the EA Explorer realm, then Users › Add user: their user name, name and email. Under Credentials, set a temporary password they change at their first sign-in.
  2. Give them their roles on the installation, in Keycloak under Role mapping: for most people user; for admins super-admin, it-admin, user-admin or auditor; model-creator if they may make models; library-admin, discovery-admin or architecture-board where they need them.
  3. Give them roles on a model: open the model, its People tab, then give the person a role: Model Admin, Model Sub Admin, Committer, Architect, History Viewer or Report Viewer.
  4. Give them roles on a project: open the project, its People tab, then give the person a role: Project Admin, Lead Architect, Architect, Model Admin, Model Sub Admin or Stakeholder.

Check it worked

System Admin › Users and roles: find the person; their EA Explorer roles, projects and models are listed. They can sign in and see what their roles allow.

If something goes wrong

  • They sign in but see almost nothing: they have no role on any model or project yet; give one (steps 3 and 4).
  • A directory group should give a role: map it once instead of giving the role to each person (see the related page).
