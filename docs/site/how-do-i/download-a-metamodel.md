## Why do this

Downloading the metamodel gives everyone a readable description of what the model holds: types, attributes, pick lists and relationships. It is also the starting point for designing a changed or new metamodel in Excel. See [[metamodels]].

A metamodel change ripples: into what people may record, what reports read, what rules check and what integrations send. That is why it goes through drafts and review, and why each version is kept and can be compared.

**In TOGAF terms:** The content framework and content metamodel: what building blocks and relationships the architecture may hold. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** A model's metamodel is in a workbook you can read and share.

## Steps

1. Open **Model Admin › Wizards**, **Download a metamodel**.
2. Pick the model; **Download**.
