## Why do this

Architecture work is iterative: a step may need another round after new information. Repeating it keeps the history of the first round, so the record shows how thinking moved. See [[the-adm]].

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

**In TOGAF terms:** Iteration within the ADM: a phase or step revisited with new information. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** A project step runs again, its earlier round kept.

> **Before you start** You need a role on the project (Architect or higher), unless a step says otherwise.

## Steps

1. Open the project, **Steps**, and the step.
2. **Do again**: say why.
3. The step is opened again; its earlier round is kept.
