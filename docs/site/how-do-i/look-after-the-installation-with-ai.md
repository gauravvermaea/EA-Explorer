## Why do this

System Admins can ask their AI assistant about health, alerts, jobs, backups and logs in plain words, and get the findings with what to look at next. It only does what your role allows. See [[ai-in-ea-explorer]].

Using AI in EA Explorer is always your assistant working with your rights: it reads and drafts, you review and commit. Used well, it takes the slow parts of architecture work (searching, cross-checking, first drafts) so you spend your time on judgement.

**In TOGAF terms:** Outside the standard's method, but in service of it: AI speeds up gathering content (phases B to D), analysis (E) and reporting to stakeholders, with people reviewing every change. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** Your AI assistant helps you check and run the installation.

> **Before you start** [Connect your AI assistant](connect-your-ai-assistant.md). You need the Super Admin or IT Admin
> role.

## Things to ask

- *How is the installation? Is anything slowing it down?*
- *Which models haven't been used this quarter?*
- *Run a housekeeping dry run and explain it.*
- *Track the number of applications on an unsupported operating system.*

Making credentials, roles or service accounts stays in the screens, never through the AI.
