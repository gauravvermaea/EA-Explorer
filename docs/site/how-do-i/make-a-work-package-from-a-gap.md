## Why do this

Work packages group the changes needed to close gaps into units that can be planned, funded and delivered. Making one from a gap keeps the line from the target architecture to the plan. See [[the-adm]].

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

**In TOGAF terms:** Phase E, Opportunities and Solutions: work packages. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** A gap becomes a piece of work in a wave.

> **Before you start** You need a role on the project (Architect or higher), unless a step says otherwise.

## Steps

1. Open the gap analysis and a gap.
2. **Work package**: name it and its wave; **Save**.
