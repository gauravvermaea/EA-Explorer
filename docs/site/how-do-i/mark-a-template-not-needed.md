## Why do this

Tailoring is normal: not every project needs every deliverable. Marking one not needed, with a reason, keeps the record honest and stops it being chased.

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

**In TOGAF terms:** The ADM itself: the phases, steps, deliverables and sign-offs of an architecture project. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** A deliverable the project will not produce is set aside, with the reason.

> **Before you start** You need a role on the project (Architect or higher), unless a step says otherwise.

## Steps

1. Open the template in the project.
2. **Won't need**: say why.
3. It moves to Templates not in use.
