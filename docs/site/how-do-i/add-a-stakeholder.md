## Why do this

Every architecture project serves people: sponsors, users, operators, regulators. Writing them down with their concerns is the first step of the ADM's Architecture Vision, and it decides which views and key questions the project needs. See [[stakeholders-and-key-questions]].

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

**In TOGAF terms:** Phase A, Architecture Vision: identifying stakeholders and their concerns. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** The project knows who it must keep informed or satisfied.

> **Before you start** You need a role on the project (Architect or higher), unless a step says otherwise.

## Steps

1. Open the project, **Stakeholders**.
2. **Add a stakeholder**: name, role and organisation.
3. Fill in their position, concerns and engagement; **Save**.
