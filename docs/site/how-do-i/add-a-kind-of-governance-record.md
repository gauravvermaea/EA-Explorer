## Why do this

The governance log holds decisions, dispensations and reviews out of the box. Many organisations have their own kinds too: a risk acceptance, a design authority ruling, an exception register. Adding the kind keeps them in the same place, searchable with the rest. See [[governance-and-sign-off]].

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

**In TOGAF terms:** Architecture governance: the governance log. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** The governance log can hold the kinds of decision your organisation actually makes.

> **Before you start** You need the Project Admin role on the project.

## Steps

1. Open the project, **Governance**.
2. **A new kind of record**: name it and say what it is for.
3. **Save**.
