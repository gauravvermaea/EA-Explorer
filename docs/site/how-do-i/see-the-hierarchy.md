## Why do this

Many architectures are hierarchies: capabilities within capabilities, processes within processes. The hierarchy shows them nested, like a capability map, so gaps and overlaps stand out.

Views and reports are where the model meets its audience. Choose the one that answers the question in front of you, for the person in front of you, and it stays current as the model changes.

**In TOGAF terms:** Architecture views and viewpoints: artifacts that address stakeholders' concerns, in any ADM phase. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** You see a model as a nested tree.

## Steps

1. Open the model, **Content › Hierarchy**.
2. Choose the type; open the levels.
3. Click an entity to open it.
