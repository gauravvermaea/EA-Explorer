## Why do this

Access reviews need to know who can do what. The users and roles page shows every person's roles and whether each came from Keycloak, a directory group or a model or project. See [[roles-and-access]].

Access in EA Explorer is layered so each owner decides for their own part: System Admins for the installation, Model Admins for models, Project Admins for projects. Most people need only a few roles to do their work.

**In TOGAF terms:** The Preliminary phase's architecture capability: who does architecture work and with what authority. Sign-in itself is outside the method. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** You see who holds a role and where it came from.

> **Before you start** You need the User Admin or Auditor role.

## Steps

1. Open **System Admin › Users and roles**.
2. Filter by role, or search for a person.
3. Open a person for every role and where it comes from.
