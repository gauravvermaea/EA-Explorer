## Why do this

An empty template has the model's own columns and allowed values. Giving it to the team behind another system means their file matches the first time, instead of failing on names and spellings. See [[bringing-data-in-and-out]].

Every route in ends the same way: what arrives is shown as new, changed, kept and problem rows, and a person decides. That review is what lets you automate feeds without losing control of the model.

**In TOGAF terms:** Supports the content of phases B, C and D (baseline architectures), keeping the Architecture Landscape current from the systems that already know it. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** Another system knows exactly what to send.

## Steps

1. Open the model, **Import and export**.
2. Choose the types; tick **Empty template**.
3. Download it as Excel or as a CSV set.
