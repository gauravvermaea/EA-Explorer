## Why do this

Project roles decide who runs the set-up, who works on content, who are stakeholders, and who signs off. Getting them right early means approvals reach the right people. See [[roles-and-access]].

Access in EA Explorer is layered so each owner decides for their own part: System Admins for the installation, Model Admins for models, Project Admins for projects. Most people need only a few roles to do their work.

**In TOGAF terms:** The Preliminary phase's architecture capability: who does architecture work and with what authority. Sign-in itself is outside the method. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** The project's people can do their part.

> **Before you start** You need the Project Admin role on the project.

## Steps

1. Open the project, **Edit**, the step **People and sign-off**.
2. **Give a person access**: find them; pick the role.
3. To remove: **Take a person's access away**.
