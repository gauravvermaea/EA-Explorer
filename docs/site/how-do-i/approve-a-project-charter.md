## Why do this

The charter is the agreement between the sponsor and the project: why it exists, what it covers, what success looks like. Approving it is the sponsor's commitment, and the point the ADM's Architecture Vision is meant to reach before detailed work starts. See [[the-adm]].

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

**In TOGAF terms:** Phase A, Architecture Vision: the sponsor's approval to proceed. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** The project has its sponsor's backing to go ahead.

> **Before you start** You are the project's sponsor, or its Project Admin or Lead Architect.

## Steps

1. Open the project, **Plan › Charter**.
2. Read the scope, goals, outcomes and what is out of scope.
3. **Approve the charter**; ask for changes first if it is not right.
