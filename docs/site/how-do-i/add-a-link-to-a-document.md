## Why do this

The repository holds the structure; the detail often lives elsewhere: a design document, a contract, a runbook. A link from the entity to that document means anyone looking at the entity can reach the detail in one click, and the link survives when the document moves into a new version.

Content work is the everyday heart of architecture: recording what exists and what should, and how it all connects. EA Explorer checks it against the metamodel as you go and keeps it as your change until it is reviewed.

**In TOGAF terms:** Phases B, C and D: recording the building blocks and relationships of the baseline and target architectures. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** An entity points to the document that explains it.

> **Before you start** You need a role on the model that may change content (Architect or higher).

## Steps

1. Open the entity and its link attribute.
2. **Add a link**: paste the address; add a note (what it is, a version).
3. **Save**.
