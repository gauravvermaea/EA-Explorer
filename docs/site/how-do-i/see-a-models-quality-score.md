## Why do this

The quality score sums up completeness, rules passed, freshness, connections and duplicates. It tells you, and others, how far to trust the model, and where to start improving. See [[data-quality]].

Quality is not a one-off clean-up but a habit: rules catch problems as they are saved, the score shows the trend, and owners fix what is theirs. A model people trust is one they keep using.

**In TOGAF terms:** Supports every ADM phase: the content framework is only useful if its building blocks are complete and correct. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** You know how trustworthy a model's data is.

## Steps

1. Open the model, **Quality**.
2. Read the score and its tiles.
3. Open a tile for the entities behind it.
