## Why do this

The skills repository shows who can do what in the practice, so work goes to the right people and training to the right gaps. See [[architecture-repository]].

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

**In TOGAF terms:** The architecture capability: skills. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** You see the architecture skills people have, and the gaps.

## Steps

1. Open **Enterprise Repository › Skills repository**.
2. Filter by skill or level.
3. People keep their own on **My profile and skills**.
