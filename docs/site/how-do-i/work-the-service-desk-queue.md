## Why do this

The service desk queue is where requests to the team that runs EA Explorer wait. Taking and closing them keeps people unblocked.

EA Explorer covers a lot of ground. A few habits (search, help on every screen, your own requests and pins) make it feel smaller and get you to your work faster.

**In TOGAF terms:** General use of the tool, across every part of the Architecture Repository. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** People's requests are answered and closed.

> **Before you start** You need the Super Admin or IT Admin role.

## Steps

1. Open **Home › Service desk › To work on**.
2. Open a request; take it.
3. Answer it; close it when done.
