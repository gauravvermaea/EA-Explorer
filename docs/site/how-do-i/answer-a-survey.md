## Why do this

Surveys gather what only people know: how mature a practice is, who uses what, what hurts. Your answers feed assessments and models; they never change a model by themselves.

Some of the most useful architecture knowledge is in people's heads. Surveys and maturity assessments gather it in a form that can be compared over time.

**In TOGAF terms:** The Preliminary phase and the architecture capability: gathering what people know and judging the maturity of the practice. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** Your knowledge reaches the people who asked for it.

## Steps

1. Open **Home › Surveys to answer**.
2. Open the survey; answer each question.
3. **Send**; you can save and come back.
