## Why do this

People live in their own calendar app, not in project tools. Subscribing puts the project's meetings and milestones there and keeps them up to date as they change, so nobody misses a workshop or a board date.

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

**In TOGAF terms:** The ADM itself: the phases, steps, deliverables and sign-offs of an architecture project. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** The project's events are in your own calendar.

## Steps

1. Open the project, **Calendar**.
2. **Subscribe to this calendar**: copy the link.
3. Add it as a subscribed calendar in your calendar app.
