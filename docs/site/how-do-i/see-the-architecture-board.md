## Why do this

The architecture board accepts the organisation's significant decisions. Its page shows its members and the records waiting for them. See [[governance-and-sign-off]].

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

**In TOGAF terms:** Architecture governance: the architecture board. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** You see the board and what waits for it.

## Steps

1. Open **Enterprise Repository › Architecture board**.
2. Read its members and the records waiting.
3. Board members accept or reject records there.
