## Why do this

The change log is the running record of what changed, by whom and why, across models. It answers "what happened last week?" and supports audits. See [[versions-tags-and-merging]].

Nothing in EA Explorer is lost: every change is kept, moments can be named, and work can happen in copies that merge back after review. This is what lets many people change one model without fear.

**In TOGAF terms:** Phase H and the Architecture Repository: change to the baseline is controlled, recorded and reversible. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** You see every committed change across the models you can see.

## Steps

1. Open **Architecture Work › Change log**.
2. Filter by model, person or date.
3. Open an entry for before and after.
