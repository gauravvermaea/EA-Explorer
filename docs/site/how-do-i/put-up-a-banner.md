> **Before you start** You need the Super Admin or IT Admin role.

## Steps

1. Open **System Admin › Settings › Banner**.
2. Write the message, choose the level (information, notice or urgent), who sees it (everyone or admins only), and if
   you like a link and the times it shows.
3. **Save**: it shows at once, and starts and ends by itself.
4. **Clear** to show nothing.
