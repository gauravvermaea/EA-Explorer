> **Before you start** You need a role on the project.

## Steps

1. The project's **Deliverables and artifacts** tab lists what the project produces; open one to see it, its versions
   and its sign-off.
2. Documents live in the project's repository: check out, change, check in as a new version.
3. The **Sign-off** tab lists what is waiting: the sign-off people approve, reject or record a decision with evidence,
   with a quorum where one is set.
4. Decisions and dispensations go in the governance log.
