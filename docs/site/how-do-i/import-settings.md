## Why do this

Importing settings saves setting up a site by hand. Every difference is shown first; you tick what to take; a safety copy is kept so Undo puts things back.

The installation is built to look after itself as far as it can, and to ask before anything risky. These tasks are the decisions it leaves to you: what to run, when, and how far to trust a change.

**In TOGAF terms:** Outside the method: this keeps the tool that holds the Architecture Repository running. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** Another site's settings are here, after you have seen every difference.

> **Before you start** You need the Super Admin role.

## Steps

1. Open **System Admin › Settings import and export**, **Import**.
2. Choose the file; read every difference.
3. Tick what to take; **Import**. A safety copy is kept; **Undo** puts it back.
