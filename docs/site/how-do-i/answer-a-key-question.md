## Why do this

Answering a key question with views, not just words, gives the stakeholder evidence they can explore. Marking it answered sends it to them to accept, which is how the project shows it served the people it was for. See [[stakeholders-and-key-questions]].

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

**In TOGAF terms:** Phases B to D: views that address stakeholders' concerns. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** A stakeholder's question has an answer and the views behind it.

> **Before you start** You need a role on the project (Architect or higher).

## Steps

1. Open the project, **Key questions**, and the question.
2. Link the views that answer it; write the answer.
3. **Mark answered**: it goes to the stakeholder to accept.
