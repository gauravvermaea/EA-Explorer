## Why do this

The wizard takes the quality findings and walks you through them, so improving a model is a guided job rather than a hunt. See [[data-quality]].

Quality is not a one-off clean-up but a habit: rules catch problems as they are saved, the score shows the trend, and owners fix what is theirs. A model people trust is one they keep using.

**In TOGAF terms:** Supports every ADM phase: the content framework is only useful if its building blocks are complete and correct. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** Missing and wrong values are fixed one entity at a time.

> **Before you start** You need a role on the model that may change content (Architect or higher).

## Steps

1. Open **Model Admin › Wizards**, **Improve data quality**.
2. Pick the findings to fix; fill in the missing values.
3. **Save**.
