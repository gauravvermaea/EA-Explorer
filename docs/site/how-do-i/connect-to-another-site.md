## Why do this

Large organisations sometimes run more than one site: by region, or for testing. Connecting lets you reach another site's models from this one without signing in separately each time.

The installation is built to look after itself as far as it can, and to ask before anything risky. These tasks are the decisions it leaves to you: what to run, when, and how far to trust a change.

**In TOGAF terms:** Outside the method: this keeps the tool that holds the Architecture Repository running. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** You can see another EA Explorer site's models from here.

## Steps

1. Open **Sites › Connect to a site**.
2. Its address and your sign-in there.
3. It is listed under **My remote instances**.
