## Why do this

Teams track work differently. Setting the statuses (to do, in progress, waiting, done or your own) makes the task list fit the team.

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

**In TOGAF terms:** The ADM itself: the phases, steps, deliverables and sign-offs of an architecture project. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** Task statuses match how your team works.

> **Before you start** You need the Project Admin role on the project.

## Steps

1. Open the project, **Tasks**, **Task statuses**.
2. Add, rename or reorder them; **Save**.
