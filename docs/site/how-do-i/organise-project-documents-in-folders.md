## Why do this

A project can gather hundreds of documents over its life. Folders, arranged the way your team thinks (by phase, by workstream, by stakeholder), keep them in order, so people find what they need without asking and new joiners can see how the work is laid out.

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

**In TOGAF terms:** The ADM itself: the phases, steps, deliverables and sign-offs of an architecture project. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** Project documents are easy to find.

> **Before you start** You need a role on the project (Architect or higher).

## Steps

1. Open the project's documents.
2. **A new folder**: name it and where it sits.
3. Move documents into it; **Rename or move** it later.
