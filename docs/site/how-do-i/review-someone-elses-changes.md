## Why do this

Committers are the model's reviewers: they look at what architects changed and commit it, or send it back saying what to fix. This is the main way a model stays right as many people work on it. See [[versions-tags-and-merging]].

Content work is the everyday heart of architecture: recording what exists and what should, and how it all connects. EA Explorer checks it against the metamodel as you go and keeps it as your change until it is reviewed.

**In TOGAF terms:** Phases B, C and D: recording the building blocks and relationships of the baseline and target architectures. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** Other people's changes are checked before they count.

> **Before you start** You need the Committer role on the model.

## Steps

1. Open the model, **Content › Changes**: everyone's uncommitted changes.
2. Open a person's changes and check them.
3. **Commit** them, or **Send back** saying what to change.
