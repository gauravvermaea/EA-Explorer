## Why do this

Checking a document in ends your exclusive work on it and keeps your file as the new version, with what changed. Earlier versions stay, so a reviewer can see what moved and a mistake can be undone.

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

**In TOGAF terms:** The ADM itself: the phases, steps, deliverables and sign-offs of an architecture project. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** Your changes to a document are kept as its new version.

> **Before you start** You need a role on the project (Architect or higher).

## Steps

1. Open the document you checked out.
2. **Check in**: choose the new file; say what changed.
3. The earlier version is kept.
