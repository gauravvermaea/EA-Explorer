## Why do this

Not every approver uses the app: some approve by email or in a meeting. Recording their decision with the evidence keeps the sign-off record complete. See [[governance-and-sign-off]].

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

**In TOGAF terms:** Architecture governance: approval of deliverables. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** A decision made outside EA Explorer is on the record, with its evidence.

> **Before you start** You need a role on the project (Architect or higher), unless a step says otherwise.

## Steps

1. Open the project, **Sign-off**, and the deliverable.
2. **Record a sign-off decision**: approved or rejected, who decided.
3. Attach the evidence (the email, the minutes); **Save**.
