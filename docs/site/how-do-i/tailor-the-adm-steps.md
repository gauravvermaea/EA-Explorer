## Why do this

The ADM is meant to be tailored. Removing steps that do not apply, with a reason, and adding your own makes the method fit the project without losing the record of why. See [[the-adm]].

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

**In TOGAF terms:** Tailoring the ADM, as the standard expects every organisation to do. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** The project runs the steps it needs, with reasons for the rest.

> **Before you start** You need the Project Admin role on the project.

## Steps

1. Open the project, **Steps**.
2. For a step that does not apply, remove it and give the reason.
3. **Add a step** to a phase; your steps are marked as the project's own.
