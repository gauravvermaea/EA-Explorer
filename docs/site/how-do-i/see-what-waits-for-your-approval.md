## Why do this

Approvals arrive from many places: deliverable sign-offs, charters, governance records, requests. One list means nothing waits on you unseen, and each item opens with its evidence. See [[governance-and-sign-off]].

EA Explorer covers a lot of ground. A few habits (search, help on every screen, your own requests and pins) make it feel smaller and get you to your work faster.

**In TOGAF terms:** General use of the tool, across every part of the Architecture Repository. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** You see everything that waits for your decision, in one list.

## Steps

1. Open **Home › Requests › Requests to me**.
2. Open an item: what it is, who asks, the evidence.
3. Decide from there, or open the page it came from.
