## Why do this

When a model grows too broad, or part of it moves to another team, splitting takes chosen types or entities, with their relationships, into a new model. The original is tagged first. See [[models]].

A model's life has stages: started, worked on, copied or split, handed over, locked or frozen at key moments, archived when done. Each stage has its wizard, and each asks before it changes anything.

**In TOGAF terms:** The Architecture Landscape in the Architecture Repository: the models at strategic, segment, capability and transition levels. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** Part of a model becomes a model of its own.

> **Before you start** You need the Model Admin role on the model.

## Steps

1. Open **Model Admin › Wizards** on the model and choose **Split part of it into a new model**.
2. Pick the entity types, or the entities, to take.
3. Choose copied or moved, and whether the new model is a child or separate.
4. Name the new model; **Split**.

## Check it worked

The new model is listed; the original is tagged first, so you can go back.
