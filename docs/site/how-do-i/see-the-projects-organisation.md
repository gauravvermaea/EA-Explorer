## Why do this

Knowing which parts of the organisation a change touches is the start of understanding who must be involved and who will be affected.

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

**In TOGAF terms:** Phase B, Business Architecture: the organisation map. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** You see the units and people a project touches.

## Steps

1. Open the project, **Business › Organisation**.
2. Read the units and roles; add the ones missing.
