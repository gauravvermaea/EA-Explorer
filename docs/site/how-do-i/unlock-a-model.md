## Why do this

A lock is meant to be short: during a review, a merge or an audit. When that moment has passed, unlocking lets architects carry on where they stopped; nothing they had in progress is lost, and the lock and its reason stay in the model's history.

A model's life has stages: started, worked on, copied or split, handed over, locked or frozen at key moments, archived when done. Each stage has its wizard, and each asks before it changes anything.

**In TOGAF terms:** The Architecture Landscape in the Architecture Repository: the models at strategic, segment, capability and transition levels. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** A locked model can be changed again.

> **Before you start** You need the Model Admin role on the model. (or Super Admin)

## Steps

1. Open **Model Admin › Wizards**, **Lock or unlock**, and pick the locked model.
2. **Unlock**.
