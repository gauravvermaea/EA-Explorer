## Why do this

The standard reports cover common questions; yours may be different. The report designer lets you pick a type, its columns, related types and filters, preview the rows, and open them in the reporting tool's chart builder. See [[views-and-reports]].

Views and reports are where the model meets its audience. Choose the one that answers the question in front of you, for the person in front of you, and it stays current as the model changes.

**In TOGAF terms:** Architecture views and viewpoints: artifacts that address stakeholders' concerns, in any ADM phase. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** A report that answers your question, as a chart or table.

## Steps

1. Open the model, **Reports › Report designer**.
2. Pick the type and its columns; add related types and filters.
3. Preview the first rows.
4. **Save and open** it in the reporting tool's chart builder.
