## Why do this

Organisations brand EA Explorer with their own colours, and some themes are hard to read for some people or screens. The standard look is always available, for you alone on this browser, so nobody is stuck with colours that do not work for them.

EA Explorer covers a lot of ground. A few habits (search, help on every screen, your own requests and pins) make it feel smaller and get you to your work faster.

**In TOGAF terms:** General use of the tool, across every part of the Architecture Repository. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** The screens are easy to read for you.

## Steps

1. Open **Home › Standard look**.
2. The standard colours are used for you, on this browser.
