## Why do this

Key questions are what stakeholders need answered to decide or act. Writing them down early focuses the project: every view it produces should answer one of them, and a question without a view shows where work is missing. See [[stakeholders-and-key-questions]].

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

**In TOGAF terms:** Phase A, Architecture Vision: stakeholders' concerns, which views will address. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** The project knows what its stakeholders need to know.

> **Before you start** You need a role on the project (Architect or higher), unless a step says otherwise.

## Steps

1. Open the project, **Key questions**.
2. Add each question and who asks it.
3. Point it at the views that answer it; **Save**.
