## Why do this

Standard views are shared by every model. Copying one into your model lets you change it (other types, other colours, another layout) without affecting anyone else. See [[views-and-reports]].

Views and reports are where the model meets its audience. Choose the one that answers the question in front of you, for the person in front of you, and it stays current as the model changes.

**In TOGAF terms:** Architecture views and viewpoints: artifacts that address stakeholders' concerns, in any ADM phase. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** A standard view becomes yours to change.

## Steps

1. Open the view.
2. **Copy as my model's view**; name it; **Save**.
3. Change the copy; other models are not affected.
