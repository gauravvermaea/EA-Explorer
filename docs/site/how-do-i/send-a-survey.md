## Why do this

Sending a survey to people, roles, models or projects puts it in their task list with a link, so it reaches exactly those who should answer.

Some of the most useful architecture knowledge is in people's heads. Surveys and maturity assessments gather it in a form that can be compared over time.

**In TOGAF terms:** The Preliminary phase and the architecture capability: gathering what people know and judging the maturity of the practice. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** The right people are asked, by a date.

> **Before you start** You need the Project Admin role.

## Steps

1. Open the survey you designed.
2. Choose who answers: people, global roles, models or projects; and by when.
3. **Send**: it reaches them in their task list and by a link.
