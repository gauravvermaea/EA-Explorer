## Why do this

Standards are the organisation's chosen technologies, formats and practices. Listing the ones a project follows makes them part of its compliance reviews and stops each solution choosing afresh. See [[principles-and-standards]].

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

**In TOGAF terms:** The Standards Information Base in the Architecture Repository. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** The project names the standards its solutions must follow.

> **Before you start** You need a role on the project (Architect or higher), unless a step says otherwise.

## Steps

1. Open the project, **Standards**.
2. **Add a standard**: pick from the library or describe your own.
3. **Save**.
