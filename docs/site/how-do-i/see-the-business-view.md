## Why do this

Business stakeholders think in capabilities, value streams and teams, not in applications and servers. The business view shows the model in those terms, without technical detail, so a conversation with the business starts from what they recognise. It reads the same model the technical views do, so the two never disagree.

Views and reports are where the model meets its audience. Choose the one that answers the question in front of you, for the person in front of you, and it stays current as the model changes.

**In TOGAF terms:** Architecture views and viewpoints: artifacts that address stakeholders' concerns, in any ADM phase. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** The model in plain business terms.

## Steps

1. Open the model, **Business**.
2. Pick what to see: capabilities, value streams, organisation.
3. Click an item to open it.
