## Why do this

Decisions forgotten get made again, often differently. Logging each one with the options considered, the reasons and the evidence means anyone later can see what was decided and why, and a dispensation carries its end date. See [[governance-and-sign-off]].

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

**In TOGAF terms:** Architecture governance: the governance log in the Architecture Repository. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** A decision, dispensation or review is recorded with its reasons.

> **Before you start** You need a role on the project (Architect or higher), unless a step says otherwise.

## Steps

1. Open the project, **Governance**.
2. **Log**: pick the kind; fill in the title, status, owner and what was decided and why.
3. **Save**, or send it for approval.
