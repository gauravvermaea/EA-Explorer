## Steps

1. Open the model, its **Views** tab: the 49 standard views, each as a diagram, catalogue, heat map, matrix and
   relationship list, and the drawn views.
2. Its **Reports** tab: the standard reports and dashboards, the **Report designer** for your own (a type, its columns,
   related types and filters), and the **OData** address for Power BI and Excel.
3. Its **KPIs** tab: the model's key figures month by month and at every tag.
4. Click any box in a picture to open the entity.
