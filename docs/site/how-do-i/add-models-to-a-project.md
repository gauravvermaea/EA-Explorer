## Why do this

A project's content lives in models: the baseline (how things are), the target (how they should be), and sometimes transitions or scenarios. Giving each its role tells EA Explorer what to compare for gaps and where most work happens. See [[projects-and-deliverables]].

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

**In TOGAF terms:** Phases B to D: the baseline and target architectures a project works on. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** The project works on the right models, each in its role.

> **Before you start** You need a role on the project (Architect or higher), unless a step says otherwise.

## Steps

1. Open the project, **Models**.
2. **Select existing models**: tick them and give each a role; **Add**.
3. Pick the **primary working model**, where most work happens.
