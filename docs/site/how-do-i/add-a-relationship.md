## Why do this

Relationships are what make a repository useful: they let you ask what an application supports, what a capability depends on, what breaks if something changes. EA Explorer only offers the relationships the metamodel allows, so connections stay meaningful. See [[metamodels]].

Content work is the everyday heart of architecture: recording what exists and what should, and how it all connects. EA Explorer checks it against the metamodel as you go and keeps it as your change until it is reviewed.

**In TOGAF terms:** Phases B, C and D: recording the building blocks and relationships of the baseline and target architectures. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** Two entities are connected in a way the metamodel allows.

> **Before you start** You need a role on the model that may change content (Architect or higher).

## Steps

1. Open the entity.
2. **Add a relationship**: pick the type (only those allowed are offered) and the other entity.
3. **Save**.
