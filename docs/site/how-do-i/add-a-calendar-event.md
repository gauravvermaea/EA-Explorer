## Why do this

Architecture projects run on meetings: workshops with the business, design reviews, steering committees, board decisions. Keeping them in the project's own calendar puts them beside the deliverables they serve, and people can see them in their own calendar app.

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

**In TOGAF terms:** The ADM itself: the phases, steps, deliverables and sign-offs of an architecture project. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** Everyone on the project knows when the workshop, review or board meeting is.

> **Before you start** You need a role on the project (Architect or higher), unless a step says otherwise.

## Steps

1. Open the project, **Calendar**.
2. **A new event**: topic, start and end, who, where; **Save**.
