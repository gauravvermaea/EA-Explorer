## Why do this

Your AI assistant can suggest a template, scope, stakeholders and key questions from a short description, and draft them for you to review. See [[ai-in-ea-explorer]].

Using AI in EA Explorer is always your assistant working with your rights: it reads and drafts, you review and commit. Used well, it takes the slow parts of architecture work (searching, cross-checking, first drafts) so you spend your time on judgement.

**In TOGAF terms:** Outside the standard's method, but in service of it: AI speeds up gathering content (phases B to D), analysis (E) and reporting to stakeholders, with people reviewing every change. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** Your AI assistant helps set up and plan a project.

> **Before you start** [Connect your AI assistant](connect-your-ai-assistant.md).

## Things to ask

- *Plan an ADM project for the claims transformation, from the transformation template.*
- *Make a scenario where we replace the Policy Admin system with the new platform, and show the impact.*
- *Check this proposed change against our principles and standards.*
- *Summarise the conflicts in this merge request and suggest a decision for each.*
