## Why do this

A heat map colours each shape by a value of its entity: cost, risk, maturity, age. It turns a diagram into a judgement: where to invest, what to retire first, which capabilities are weak. It is one of the most persuasive views for a decision-maker. See [[views-and-reports]].

Views and reports are where the model meets its audience. Choose the one that answers the question in front of you, for the person in front of you, and it stays current as the model changes.

**In TOGAF terms:** Architecture views and viewpoints: artifacts that address stakeholders' concerns, in any ADM phase. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** Where it is worst shows at a glance.

## Steps

1. Open the view.
2. **Heat map**: pick the attribute and the ranges.
3. The legend explains the colours.
