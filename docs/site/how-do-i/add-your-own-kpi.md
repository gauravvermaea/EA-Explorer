## Why do this

KPIs show how the architecture changes over time: how many applications are past end of life, how much cost sits in duplicated capabilities. Your own KPI counts or sums what matters to your organisation, kept every month and at every tag so the trend is visible. See [[views-and-reports]].

Views and reports are where the model meets its audience. Choose the one that answers the question in front of you, for the person in front of you, and it stays current as the model changes.

**In TOGAF terms:** Architecture views and viewpoints: artifacts that address stakeholders' concerns, in any ADM phase. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** A figure you care about is kept month by month.

> **Before you start** You need the Model Admin role on the model.

## Steps

1. Open the model, **Reports › KPIs**.
2. Add a KPI: what it counts or sums, and its filter.
3. **Save**; it is kept on the 1st of each month and at every tag.
