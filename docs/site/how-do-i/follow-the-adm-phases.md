## Why do this

The ADM gives a project its shape: each phase has its steps and deliverables. Following the phases shows what is done, what is in progress and what is next, so the project neither skips nor repeats work. See [[the-adm]].

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

**In TOGAF terms:** The ADM: phases Preliminary and A to H, with Requirements Management at the centre. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** You know where the project is in the ADM and what comes next.

## Steps

1. Open the project, **Plan › Phases**.
2. Pick a phase: its steps and deliverables.
3. Open a step to work on it.
