## Why do this

Principles are the lasting rules behind architecture decisions. Adding one to a project makes it part of what the project must respect, what compliance reviews check, and what a dispensation is measured against. See [[principles-and-standards]].

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

**In TOGAF terms:** The Preliminary phase's architecture principles, applied in phase A and every phase after. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** The project follows an agreed principle, and its decisions can be checked against it.

> **Before you start** You need a role on the project (Architect or higher), unless a step says otherwise.

## Steps

1. Open the project, **Principles**.
2. **Add a principle**: pick from the library, or write the statement, rationale and implications.
3. **Save**.
