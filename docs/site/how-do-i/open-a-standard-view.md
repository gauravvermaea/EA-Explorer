## Why do this

Standard views answer common architecture questions, each as a diagram, catalogue, heat map, matrix or relationship list. They are the quickest way to see what a model holds. See [[views-and-reports]].

Views and reports are where the model meets its audience. Choose the one that answers the question in front of you, for the person in front of you, and it stays current as the model changes.

**In TOGAF terms:** Architecture views and viewpoints: artifacts that address stakeholders' concerns, in any ADM phase. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** A ready-made view of the model, in the form you need.

## Steps

1. Open the model, **Views**, **Standard views**.
2. Pick a view.
3. Switch between diagram, catalogue, heat map, matrix and relationships.
