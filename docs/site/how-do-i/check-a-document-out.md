## Why do this

Documents are edited outside EA Explorer, in Office. Checking one out tells everyone it is yours for now, so two people do not change the same document and lose each other's work.

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

**In TOGAF terms:** The ADM itself: the phases, steps, deliverables and sign-offs of an architecture project. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** You can work on a document without someone else changing it at the same time.

> **Before you start** You need a role on the project (Architect or higher).

## Steps

1. Open the document.
2. **Check out**; it downloads.
3. Change it on your machine.
