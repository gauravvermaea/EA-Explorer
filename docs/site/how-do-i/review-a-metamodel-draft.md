## Why do this

A metamodel change changes what everyone may record, so a second pair of eyes is worth it: does the type make sense, is the attribute needed, will the reports still work? See [[metamodels]].

A metamodel change ripples: into what people may record, what reports read, what rules check and what integrations send. That is why it goes through drafts and review, and why each version is kept and can be compared.

**In TOGAF terms:** The content framework and content metamodel: what building blocks and relationships the architecture may hold. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** A metamodel change is checked before it applies to everyone.

> **Before you start** You need the Model Admin role on the model.

## Steps

1. Open **Model Admin › Metamodel drafts** and the draft.
2. Read the summary; **Comment on the draft** if you need to.
3. **Accept the metamodel changes**, or **Reject** with a reason.
