## Architecture is for someone

A diagram nobody asked for is rarely read. Good architecture work starts from the people with a stake in the change,
what worries them, and what they need to know to decide. Those needs are the **key questions** ("Which applications
will be affected by closing the Durban office?", "What does the new claims process cost us in systems?"), and every
view the project produces should answer one of them.

## How EA Explorer keeps them

Each project lists its **stakeholders** with their role, position, concerns and how much power and interest they
have, which tells you how to engage each ([[map-stakeholders]]). Each **key question** names who asks it and the views
that answer it; when answered, it goes back to that stakeholder to accept ([[answer-a-key-question]],
[[accept-an-answer]]). A question with no view, or a stakeholder with no question, is a sign something is missing.
