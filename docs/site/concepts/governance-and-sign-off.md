## Why governance

Architecture is only useful if it is followed, and only followed if the decisions behind it are clear and fairly made.
Governance is the practice of recording those decisions, checking work against them, and agreeing exceptions openly
rather than letting them happen quietly. It protects the people making hard calls as much as the organisation.

## What EA Explorer records

- **The governance log**: decisions (with the options and reasons), dispensations (agreed exceptions, with an end
  date), compliance reviews and any records of your own kinds. See [[log-a-governance-record]] and [[read-the-governance-log]].
- **Sign-off**: each deliverable's approval or rejection, who decided, when, and the evidence (an email, the minutes).
  A project has sign-off people and a quorum. See [[record-a-sign-off]].
- **The architecture board**: the group that accepts the organisation's records ([[see-the-architecture-board]]).
- **History**: every committed change to a model, by whom and why ([[see-the-change-log]]).

For an executive, this is where approvals arrive and where the reasons behind a recommendation can be read.
