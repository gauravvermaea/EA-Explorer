## Why start from a reference

Most organisations in an industry share much of their shape: every insurer handles policies, claims and reinsurance;
every bank has accounts, payments and lending. An industry model captures that shared shape (capabilities, value
streams, information, and a metamodel tuned to the industry), so a new model starts from a sound, complete structure
rather than a blank page, and organisations can compare themselves with a common reference.

## How they are made and shared

Industry model makers build a model and its metamodel ([[build-a-metamodel-in-excel]]), then **promote** it to the
library, ask for it to be **published**, and a Library Admin approves it ([[publish-a-reference-model]]). Published
models appear in the model library and, with their files, as industry packs ([[use-an-industry-pack]]).

## How you use one

Copy it to start your own ([[copy-a-model-from-the-library]]), or make a child of it so later improvements to the
reference can flow to you ([[make-a-child-model]]).
