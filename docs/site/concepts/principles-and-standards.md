## Why principles

A principle is a short, lasting rule that guides decisions: "Data is an asset", "Buy before build", "Reuse before
buy". Each has a statement, the reasoning behind it, and what it implies in practice. Written down and agreed, they
let many people make consistent decisions without asking the same questions every time, and they give a fair basis
for saying no.

**Standards** are more specific: the technologies, products, formats and practices the organisation has chosen.

## How EA Explorer uses them

The organisation's principles live in a library ([[principles-library]]); each project picks the ones it follows and
may add or refine its own ([[add-a-principle-to-a-project]], [[refine-a-principle]]). Standards are added the same
way ([[add-a-standard]]). Compliance reviews check a solution against both ([[run-a-compliance-review]]), and an
agreed exception is recorded as a dispensation in the governance log. See [[governance-and-sign-off]].
