## The short version

An organisation is a web of things that depend on each other: the capabilities it needs, the processes that deliver
them, the people and teams who run them, the applications and data they use, the technology underneath, and the
suppliers, rules and money around all of it. Enterprise architecture is the discipline of keeping a reliable picture
of that web, so that decisions about change are made with the whole in view rather than one part at a time.

Without it, the same questions are answered again and again from scratch: what does this system support, who uses it,
what breaks if we switch it off, what will the new strategy actually change? With it, those answers are already
there, kept up to date, and can be trusted because everyone works from the same picture.

## What the picture is made of

The picture is a set of **models**: each model holds **entities** (a capability, an application, a process, a
person's role) and the **relationships** between them (this application serves that process). What kinds of entity
and relationship a model may hold is set by its **metamodel**: see [[metamodels]] and [[models]].

From the models come **views**: diagrams, catalogues, matrices and heat maps that answer one question for one
audience. See [[views-and-reports]].

## How the picture changes

The picture changes in two ways. Day to day, architects keep it current: a new application goes live, a team
reorganises. And through **architecture projects**, which take a deliberate route from how things are to how they
should be, following the TOGAF Architecture Development Method: see [[the-adm]] and [[kinds-of-projects]].

## Where EA Explorer fits

EA Explorer is the repository where all of this lives: the models and their metamodels, the views and reports, the
projects with their deliverables and sign-offs, the principles and standards, and the history of every change. It is
open source and you run it yourself, so the picture of your organisation stays with you. Your own AI assistant can
read and work on it too: see [[ai-in-ea-explorer]].
