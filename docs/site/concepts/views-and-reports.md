## A view answers one question for one audience

A model can hold thousands of entities; nobody reads it whole. A **view** selects what one audience needs to see to
answer one question, and shows it the way they read best: a diagram for structure, a catalogue for a list, a matrix
for "what touches what", a heat map for "where is it worst". Because views read the model, they are always as current
as the model and never contradict each other.

## What EA Explorer offers

- **49 standard views** in five forms each: diagram, catalogue, heat map, matrix and relationships
  ([[open-a-standard-view]]). Copy one to make it your own ([[copy-a-view-into-your-model]]).
- **Drawn views**: diagrams you lay out by hand in draw.io, each shape bound to a real entity ([[draw-a-view]]).
- **Graphs**: an entity's network and its impact, many steps out ([[see-the-impact-of-a-change]]).
- **Reports and dashboards**: 30 standard ones, and your own from the report designer ([[build-your-own-report]]).
- **KPIs**: the model's key figures, kept every month and at every tag, to show the trend ([[see-the-model-kpis]]).
- **Outside tools**: Power BI or Excel read the model live ([[read-a-model-in-power-bi]]).

For an executive, the dashboards and KPIs give the state of the estate at a glance, and a project's report gives
where each deliverable stands.
