## What it shows

**Rows:** Capability. **Columns:** Goal, Outcome, Course Of Action.

## Why it matters

A matrix, in the TOGAF Standard's content framework, shows how two kinds of building block relate: rows of one kind, columns of the other, a mark where they are connected. It answers "what touches what" at a glance.

**In TOGAF terms:** A matrix of the TOGAF content framework (phase: A and B). See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

## Who it is for

CEO, COO, Business architect.

## How to use it

Use it as the TOGAF Strategy and capability matrix: which items of one kind relate to which of another.

## How to read it

A filled cell is a relationship between the row and the column. Empty rows or columns are items with no link to the other kind; very full rows or columns are hubs.

## Where to find it

Open a model, **Reports › Reports**, and choose **Strategy and capability matrix**; it opens its dashboard for that model. Every chart can be filtered, and most rows have **Open** to go to the entity in EA Explorer. The same data is in the model's OData feed for your own tools ([[odata-feeds]]).

For developers and report builders: it reads `rpt.type_pairs`; its dashboard is `eax-tm08`.
