## What it shows

From the ArchiMate 4 Specification, Appendix C (The Open Group).

**Its concerns:** The resources of the enterprise, the capabilities they serve and the work packages that change them. **For:** Business Managers, Enterprise and Business Architects. **Purpose:** Designing, deciding.

**Element types shown:** Resource, Capability, Work Package, Grouping.

**Relationships shown:** Composition, Aggregation, Assignment, Realization, Serving, Access, Influence, Association, Triggering, Flow, Specialization. The heat map colours by **Criticality**.

## Why it matters

Views from the ArchiMate 4 Specification each serve a set of stakeholders and concerns, by showing only the element and relationship types that matter to them. EA Explorer shows each one from your model as a catalogue, a matrix, a network and a heat map.

**In TOGAF terms:** An architecture view, addressing stakeholders' concerns; it usually supports the TOGAF artifact named below. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

## Who it is for

COO, CFO, Portfolio manager, Business architect.

## How to use it

Use it to look at the model the way the Resource Map view's stakeholders need: only the element and relationship types that matter to their concerns.

## How to read it

The catalog lists the elements, the relationships table shows how they connect, and the heat map colours elements by the view's attribute. Missing elements of a type mean that part is not modelled yet.

## Where to find it

Open a model, **Reports › Reports**, and choose **Resource Map view**; it opens its dashboard for that model. Every chart can be filtered, and most rows have **Open** to go to the entity in EA Explorer. The same data is in the model's OData feed for your own tools ([[odata-feeds]]).

For developers and report builders: it reads `rpt.vp_elements`; its dashboard is `eax-vp22`.
