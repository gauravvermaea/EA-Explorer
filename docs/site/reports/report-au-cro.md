## What it shows

**What it gathers:** [[report-sr-17|SR-17 Technology lifecycle and end of life]], [[report-sr-21|SR-21 Vendor concentration]], [[report-sr-23|SR-23 Obsolescence risk]], [[report-sr-24|SR-24 Single points of failure]], [[report-vp-35|VP-35 Process/Event/Control/Product catalog view]], [[report-dr-03|DR-03 Impact chain from technology to value stream]], [[report-dr-04|DR-04 Dependency counts and hot spots]].

## Why it matters

An audience dashboard gathers, on one page, the headline charts of the reports one kind of person usually needs, so they start from what matters to them instead of browsing the whole catalogue.

**In TOGAF terms:** Stakeholder management: each audience sees the views that address its concerns. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

## Who it is for

Chief Risk Officer.

## How to use it

Use it as the starting dashboard for the Chief Risk Officer dashboard: the reports that matter to this audience, together.

## How to read it

Each chart is described where it comes from; open the full report from the Reports tab for detail.

## Where to find it

Open a model, **Reports › Reports**, and choose **Chief Risk Officer dashboard**; it opens its dashboard for that model. Every chart can be filtered, and most rows have **Open** to go to the entity in EA Explorer. The same data is in the model's OData feed for your own tools ([[odata-feeds]]).
