## Why it matters

A catalogue, in the TOGAF Standard's content framework, is a structured list of one kind of building block with its key attributes: the reference others check against. This one belongs to the ADM phase shown above.

**In TOGAF terms:** A catalogue of the TOGAF content framework (phase: B: business). See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

## Who it is for

Product owner, Business unit head, Business architect.

## How to use it

Use it as the TOGAF product and service catalogue: the list to review with its owners and keep current, filtered by model.

## How to read it

Each row is one item with its key attributes; blanks are data to complete. Open goes to the item in EA Explorer.

## Where to find it

Open a model, **Reports › Reports**, and choose **Product and service catalogue**; it opens its dashboard for that model. Every chart can be filtered, and most rows have **Open** to go to the entity in EA Explorer. The same data is in the model's OData feed for your own tools ([[odata-feeds]]).

For developers and report builders: it reads `rpt.tc_products`; its dashboard is `eax-tc06`.
