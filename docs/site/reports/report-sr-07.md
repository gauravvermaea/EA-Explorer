## Why it matters

The standard reports answer the questions most architecture practices are asked again and again: what we have, what it costs, how it depends on what, and where the risks are. They work on any model built on a metamodel with the matching types and attributes.

**In TOGAF terms:** Architecture views and catalogues that address common stakeholder concerns, in phases B to F. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

## Who it is for

Enterprise architect, Change manager.

## How to use it

Use it to list who is involved: actors, roles, collaborations and stakeholders, for engagement planning.

## How to read it

Stakeholders with no concerns recorded, and actors with no role, are the ones to follow up.

## Where to find it

Open a model, **Reports › Reports**, and choose **Stakeholder and actor catalogue**; it opens its dashboard for that model. Every chart can be filtered, and most rows have **Open** to go to the entity in EA Explorer. The same data is in the model's OData feed for your own tools ([[odata-feeds]]).

For developers and report builders: it reads `rpt.sr07_actors`; its dashboard is `eax-sr07`.
