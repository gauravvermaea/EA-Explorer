## Why do this

Deliverables are what a project leaves behind; sign-off is the proof they were agreed. See [[projects-and-deliverables]] and [[governance-and-sign-off]].

A project carries one pass of the ADM from vision to sign-off. Each task here is one of the steps along that route, and the project keeps them together so the whole story can be read later.

**In TOGAF terms:** The ADM itself: the phases, steps, deliverables and sign-offs of an architecture project. See [[ea-explorer-and-togaf]].

**The goal:** Every deliverable is produced, versioned and signed off.

> **Before you start** You need a role on the project.

## The ways to do it

1. The project's **Deliverables and artifacts** tab lists what the project produces; open one to see it, its versions
   and its sign-off.
2. Documents live in the project's repository: check out, change, check in as a new version.
3. The **Sign-off** tab lists what is waiting: the sign-off people approve, reject or record a decision with evidence,
   with a quorum where one is set.
4. Decisions and dispensations go in the governance log.
