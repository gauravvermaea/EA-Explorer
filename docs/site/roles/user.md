## Who this is for

Everyone who works with the architecture day to day: **Architects** (change content), **Committers** (review and
commit changes), **History Viewers** and **Report Viewers** (read and report), and the people on projects
(**Architect**, **Stakeholder**). What you may do depends on your roles on each model and project: what you may not use
is not shown.

## What you have

| Area | Where | What it gives you |
|---|---|---|
| Your work | Architecture Work › My projects, My models | The projects and models you work in, with what needs you |
| Finding | **/** or Ctrl+K anywhere; Me › Search everything | Any function, model, project or entity by name, ID, description or value; near spellings work |
| A model | Open a model | **Grid** (a table you edit like a spreadsheet), **Data entry** (one entity at a time in a form), **Views**, **Quality**, **Reports**, **KPIs**, **Changes** (uncommitted), **History** and **Tags** |
| Views | A model's Views tab | 49 standard views (diagram, catalogue, heat map, matrix, relationships), networks, impact, capability maps, roadmaps, and drawn views in the built-in draw.io |
| Reports | A model's Reports tab | 30 standard reports and dashboards, the report designer for your own, and the OData address for Power BI and Excel |
| Compare | Architecture Work › Compare and analyse | Two models or two moments compared; gap analysis; common analysis across many models |
| Projects | Open a project | Its phases and steps, models, deliverables, stakeholders, key questions and documents |
| Your tasks | Me › My tasks, Notifications, Surveys to answer | Requests, approvals, reviews and surveys waiting for you |
| AI | AI (MCP) | Connect your own AI assistant; it works as you, with your permissions |
| Help | **?** or F1 | Help for what is on the screen, every document, and this site |

## How it is put together

- **Entities and relationships**: every element of the architecture (a capability, a process, an application, a
  server...) is an entity of a type; relationships join them, only in the ways the metamodel allows. Each entity has an
  ID, attributes, links to documents, a history and the views it appears in.
- **Your changes are private until committed**: what you add or change is your uncommitted change, seen by you and the
  model's Committers, until it is committed. A Committer reviews and commits it, or sends it back.
- **Views are drawn from the repository** when you open them, so they always show the data as it is now. A drawn view
  you make in draw.io stays in step with the model: a relationship you draw goes into the model as your change.
- **History**: every change is kept, so you can see any entity or model as it was at any moment or tag.
